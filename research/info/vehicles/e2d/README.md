# E-2D Advanced Hawkeye carrier airborne early warning aircraft

## Dimensions and mass
- Length: 57.7 ft (17.6 m).
- Wingspan: 80.6 ft (24.6 m); wings fold for carrier stowage (fold point roughly two-thirds out, folding aft alongside the fuselage).
- Height: 18.5 ft (5.6 m) (tall due to the tail fin arrangement and rotodome mast).
- Rotodome diameter: 24 ft (7.3 m), mounted above the fuselage on a mast/pylon, tilted/rotated during flight.
- Empty weight: ~40,300 lb (18,300 kg); max takeoff weight: ~57,700 lb (26,172 kg).
- Speed: max ~325 kt (374 mph, 602 km/h); cruise ~300 kt.
- Crew: 5 (2 pilots + 3 mission-system operators in the rear cabin).
Sources: [Wikipedia, Northrop Grumman E-2 Hawkeye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman_E-2_Hawkeye), [Navy fact file, E-2D Advanced Hawkeye](https://www.navy.mil/Resources/Fact-Files/Display-FactFiles/Article/2169892/e-2d-advanced-hawkeye/), [Northrop Grumman E-2D page](https://www.northropgrumman.com/what-we-do/air/e-2d-advanced-hawkeye/).

## Speed / propulsion
- 2x Rolls-Royce (Allison) T56-A-427A turboprop engines, 8-blade (NP2000) composite propellers, ~5,100 shp each.
Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman_E-2_Hawkeye).

## Shape and layout (nose to tail)
- **Nose**: rounded, glazed cockpit windows, small radome tip housing weather/nav radar (separate from the main rotodome sensor).
- **Cockpit**: high-mounted flight deck, two pilots side by side.
- **Fuselage/cabin**: short, stubby fuselage relative to the wingspan; rear cabin houses the 3 mission-system operator stations (no windows along most of the cabin — instruments-only workstations).
- **Rotodome**: the aircraft's defining feature — a large disc-shaped radome mounted on a pylon above the fuselage, roughly mid-body, housing the AN/APY-9 mechanically rotating + electronically scanned radar array; rotates continuously in flight (typically 6 rpm class for legacy Hawkeye rotodomes; the APY-9 can also operate in a slower/non-rotating electronic-scan sector mode — exact modern rotation rate not fully published).
- **Wings**: high-mounted, straight (not swept), with distinctive large endplate-style twin vertical tail fins plus a small central vertical fin — four vertical surfaces total (two large outboard fins at the tailplane tips, one smaller central fin, sometimes described as tri-tail/quad-tail depending on count convention) needed for stability given the rotodome's aerodynamic disturbance.
- **Horizontal tailplane**: mounted at the rear fuselage, carries the multiple vertical fins.
- **Engines**: 2x turboprops mounted on the wings, one per side, well outboard of the fuselage, with 8-blade composite propellers (a modern retrofit over the older 4-blade prop of earlier E-2 variants).
- **Undercarriage**: fixed-appearing but retractable tricycle gear (nose gear well forward, main gear under the wings/fuselage), tailhook under the rear fuselage for carrier recovery.
- **Wing fold**: outer wing panels fold aft/up for hangar stowage aboard carriers, a hallmark of all carrier-based Hawkeye/Greyhound-family aircraft.

## Moving parts
- Rotodome rotates continuously in flight — historically ~6 rpm on earlier E-2C AN/APS-145; the APY-9 radar on the E-2D can also run in an electronically-scanned "stare" mode without full mechanical rotation for parts of its scan — exact current rotation rate/mode timing is not publicly detailed, treat 6 rpm as the legacy reference figure only.
- 8-blade NP2000 propellers, standard turboprop cruise RPM (not publicly itemized per aircraft).
- Wings fold hydraulically for deck stowage.
- Tailhook extends for carrier landings.

## Markings / colours
- Standard US Navy light/dark grey low-visibility tactical scheme, squadron tail codes on the vertical fins, "NAVY" stencil, side number on the nose.

Note: exact rotodome rotation rate and radar scan-mode timing for the AN/APY-9 are not publicly published (partly classified); the 6 rpm figure is the well-documented legacy E-2C value cited here for reference only, not a confirmed E-2D figure.
