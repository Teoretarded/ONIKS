- [ResearchGate: Nimitz-class flight deck layout diagram](https://www.researchgate.net/figure/The-layout-plan-of-a-Nimitz-class-aircraft-carriers-flight-deck_fig1_331402333) — schematic flight-deck plan (catapults, elevators, wires).
- [Library of Congress: revised Nimitz-class design drawings](https://www.loc.gov/item/2020631650) — historical U.S. Navy design drawings, scanned originals.
- [usmilitaryart.com: profile drawings of Nimitz-class carriers by George Bieda](https://www.usmilitaryart.com/Aircraft+Carriers-18/Profile+Drawings+of+Nimitz+Class+Aircraft+Carriers+by+George+Bieda-629) — side-profile line art (not free-licence, reference only).
- [Wikipedia, Nimitz-class aircraft carrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nimitz-class_aircraft_carrier) — has a labelled cutaway/plan diagram.
