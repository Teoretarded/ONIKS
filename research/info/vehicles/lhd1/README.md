# LHD-1 Wasp-class amphibious assault ship

## Dimensions and mass
- Length overall: 843 ft (257 m).
- Beam: 106 ft (32.3 m) (waterline); flight deck overhangs wider.
- Draft: ~26.4–27.6 ft (8.1–8.4 m) (full load, varies by source).
- Displacement: ~40,500–41,000 long tons full load.
- Crew: ~1,100 ship's company + up to ~1,900 embarked Marines (~3,000 total with full Marine Expeditionary Unit load).
Sources: [Wikipedia, Wasp-class amphibious assault ship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wasp-class_amphibious_assault_ship), [NAVSEA/Navy fact file, amphibious assault ships](https://www.navy.mil/Resources/Fact-Files/Display-FactFiles/).

## Speed / propulsion
- 2x steam boilers, 2x geared steam turbines, 2 shafts, ~70,000 shp.
- Max speed: 20+ kt (officially "in excess of 20 knots"; commonly cited ~22 kt).
Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wasp-class_amphibious_assault_ship).

## Shape and layout (bow to stern)
- **Bow**: full-form, blunt bow (cargo-ship-like hull, not a warship's fine entry) — flight deck overhangs it.
- **Forward flight deck**: 9 helicopter landing spots run the length of the flight deck (which spans nearly the whole ship, offset to port).
- **Island**: starboard side, roughly amidships to slightly aft, tall multi-deck superstructure carrying the bridge, primary flight control, air-search radar (SPS-48/49 type), and the ship's stacks integrated into the island (funnel uptakes routed through it, similar arrangement logic to a carrier).
- **Self-defense weapons**: originally 2x Mk 29 NATO Sea Sparrow launchers and 2x Phalanx CIWS (bow and stern sponsons/positions), many hulls updated with RAM (RIM-116) launchers replacing Sea Sparrow — positions/fit vary by hull and refit era.
- **Hangar deck**: large enclosed hangar bay below the flight deck running most of midships length, aircraft elevators (2x, deck-edge, one forward-starboard, one aft-port typical arrangement) move aircraft between hangar and flight deck.
- **Well deck**: aft third of the ship, opens via a stern gate that lowers into the water — floods to launch/recover LCACs (fits up to 3 LCAC air-cushion craft) or conventional landing craft (LCU/LCM/AAV).
- **Stern**: stern gate/ramp for the well deck, flight deck continues over the well deck at the very aft end (spot 9).
- **Vehicle/cargo decks**: below the hangar, forward of the well deck, store Marine vehicles and cargo, served by ramps and a side port for loading.

## Moving parts
- 2 side/deck-edge aircraft elevators cycle between hangar and flight deck.
- Stern gate lowers to flood/drain the well deck (ballasting the stern down several feet to make the well deck operable) — a multi-minute ballast/deballast evolution, not instantaneous.
- Air-search radar rotates atop the island mast, typical shipboard rate ~6 rpm class (not officially published per-hull).
- Can operate AV-8B Harrier STOVL jets (no catapults/arresting gear — vertical/short takeoff only) alongside helicopters and, on some hulls modified as "Lightning Carriers," F-35B.

## Markings / colours
- Haze grey hull/superstructure, flight deck marked with 9 numbered landing spots (white/yellow circles and spot numbers), hull number ("LHD-1") in white on the bow, dark non-skid flight-deck coating.

Note: displacement and draft figures vary somewhat between the Navy fact file and secondary sources depending on load condition measured; self-defense weapon fit (Sea Sparrow vs RAM, CIWS count/position) differs by hull and refit — treat the description above as a composite/typical Wasp-class fit rather than one specific hull's exact loadout.
