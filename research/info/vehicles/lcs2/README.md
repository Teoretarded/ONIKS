# LCS-2 Independence-class littoral combat ship (trimaran)

## Dimensions and mass
- Length overall: 418.9 ft (127.6 m).
- Beam (max, at the aft sponsons/outriggers): 103.7 ft (31.6 m) — extremely wide relative to length for a trimaran hull.
- Draft: ~14.8 ft (4.5 m) (shallow for a combatant, littoral-optimized).
- Displacement: ~3,100–3,500 long tons full load (~2,784 LT light).
- Crew: ~40 core crew + mission-module/aviation detachment, up to ~104 total with full complement.
Sources: [Wikipedia, Independence-class littoral combat ship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence-class_littoral_combat_ship), [NAVSEA LCS fact sheet](https://www.navsea.navy.mil/).

## Speed / propulsion
- Combined diesel and gas (CODAG) plant: 2x GE LM2500 gas turbines + 2x diesel engines driving 4 waterjets.
- Max speed: 40+ kt (commonly cited ~44 kt on trials).
- Range: ~4,300 nmi at 14–18 kt economical speed (varies by source).
Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence-class_littoral_combat_ship).

## Shape and layout (bow to stern)
- **Bow**: slender, sharply raked center hull (the aluminium trimaran main hull is much narrower than the overall beam — the outriggers/side hulls only touch water near the stern).
- **Forward gun**: Mk 110 57mm gun, bow mount, low-profile stealth-shaped housing.
- **Forward superstructure**: low, angular, radar-faceted deckhouse with slab sides for RCS reduction; integrated mast carries the SeaRAM/air-search sensors.
- **Bridge**: enclosed bridge within the forward superstructure, tall for visibility over the wide beam.
- **Mission bay**: the ship's defining feature — a huge reconfigurable internal bay (~11,000 sq ft / ~1,030 m² across two decks) amidships/aft for swappable mission modules (mine countermeasures, surface warfare, ASW packages), served by a side port/ramp.
- **Outrigger side hulls (trimaran sponsons)**: flare out from the center hull about amidships, widening the ship dramatically and giving huge stability and deck area for the beam; they run aft to the stern, visually the most distinctive recognition feature versus any monohull.
- **Flight deck**: very large aft flight deck (larger than most frigates/destroyers, sized for two MH-60 helicopters or one plus UAVs) spanning the full beam at the stern.
- **Hangar**: enclosed hangar structure at the aft end of the superstructure, forward of the flight deck, sized for 2 MH-60R/S or 1 helo + Fire Scout UAS.
- **SeaRAM**: self-defense missile/CIWS mount, typically atop the hangar structure aft, above the flight deck.
- **Stern**: transom stern with a stern ramp/launch-and-recovery system for small boats and unmanned vehicles, waterjet outlets below.

## Moving parts
- 4x waterjets (no visible propellers/rudders) — steering by vectoring jet nozzles, very tight turning radius for the size.
- Stern ramp lowers/raises for boat and UUV/USV launch and recovery.
- SeaRAM mount trains/elevates continuously scanning; no large rotating radar dish — sensors are panel/faceted arrays on the mast.
- Side mission-bay door/ramp opens for module and vehicle transfer.

## Markings / colours
- Haze grey hull, low-contrast/low-visibility hull-number stencils (small, grey-on-grey, unlike traditional bold white hull numbers) consistent with the stealth design language; flight deck marked with standard yellow/white landing circle and deck markings.

Note: exact displacement and top-speed figures vary between the Independence-class Wikipedia/Navy figures depending on measurement (light vs. full load, trial vs. sustained speed) — treat 40–44 kt and 3,100–3,500 LT as the credible ranges, not single fixed numbers.
