# Kornet-EM (9P163-3) on Tigr-M (GAZ-233114) 4x4

Anti-tank guided missile system: retractable twin missile launcher pod + sensor mast, mounted on the armoured
Tigr-M light multipurpose vehicle. Kornet-EM missiles (9M133M-2 family) have laser-beam-riding guidance,
tandem-HEAT or thermobaric warheads, ranges out to ~8-10 km (vs ~5.5 km for baseline Kornet).

## Dimensions and mass (Tigr-M / GAZ-233114 chassis)

| | |
|---|---|
| Length | ~5.7 m |
| Width | ~2.3 m |
| Height (hull, launcher stowed) | ~2.4 m; mast/launcher raised extends well above roofline |
| Wheelbase | ~3.3 m (4x4) |
| Combat mass | ~10-12 t (armoured Tigr-M with Kornet-EM turret) |
| Road speed | up to 100-120 km/h |
| Range | ~800-1,000 km (chassis fuel range) |
| Crew | 2 (some sources cite up to 3) |
| Deploy time | launcher raises from stowed to firing position in well under a minute (not officially timed) |

Sources: [Wikipedia: Kornet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9M133_Kornet), [Army Recognition: Kornet-EM data sheet](https://www.armyrecognition.com/russia_russian_missile_system_vehicle_uk/kornet-em_9p163-3_tracked_wheeled_anti-tank_guided_missile_system_technical_data_sheet_specifications_pictures.html), [Military Today: Tigr](https://www.military-today.com/apc/tigr.htm). Mass and crew figures vary between the base Tigr-M and Kornet-EM-fitted variants; not all sources distinguish them clearly.

## Shape and layout

Tigr-M base vehicle: boxy armoured 4x4 hull, sloped glacis front, flat armoured windscreen with wipers, large
single-piece doors, run-flat tyres on visible independent-suspension arms. Kornet-EM turret/launcher module
replaces the standard roof hatch/cargo area: a remotely-operated mast rises from the vehicle's rear-centre
roof, topped by a twin-tube launcher (two ready missile tubes side by side) and, above/behind it, a combined
sensor head (thermal imager, day camera, laser rangefinder/designator) — the whole assembly retracts down
flush with the roofline for travel and telescopes/rises up for engagement, allowing the vehicle to fire from
defilade (hull hidden, only the mast head exposed).

## Moving parts

- **Telescoping mast**: raises the launcher+sensor head above the vehicle roofline for firing, retracts for
  travel; can be operated remotely from inside the armoured cabin.
- **Twin launch tubes**: elevate/traverse with the mast head to track and engage targets; reload requires
  crew to expose themselves or use an automated magazine on some variants (not confirmed for this specific
  fit).

## Colours / camouflage

Standard Russian MoD olive-drab or digital camouflage on the Tigr-M hull; launcher mast typically matches
hull colour. See `images/` for parade (solid colour) and exercise (some camouflage) examples.

## Disagreements / gaps

Exact combat mass and deploy/retract timing for the Kornet-EM-on-Tigr fit specifically are not publicly
documented — figures above are inferred from the base Tigr-M chassis and general Kornet-EM system
descriptions. Only a small number of clear photos of this specific combination (turret closed vs. erected)
were found on Commons; most available Kornet-EM photos show the system on its towed/static tripod mount or
the tracked 9P163M-1 platform rather than the Tigr-M pickup, so verify silhouette against the video links
before finalising the in-game model.
