# Kornet-EM on Tigr-M — drawings and video links

## Drawings / blueprints

- [Army Recognition Kornet-EM data sheet](https://www.armyrecognition.com/russia_russian_missile_system_vehicle_uk/kornet-em_9p163-3_tracked_wheeled_anti-tank_guided_missile_system_technical_data_sheet_specifications_pictures.html) — photos of both tracked and wheeled variants.
- [Military Today Tigr page](https://www.military-today.com/apc/tigr.htm) — base-chassis profile diagram (unarmed Tigr-M).
- No dedicated Kornet-EM-on-Tigr scale-model kit found; use Tigr-M armoured-car kits (widely available) for
  hull proportions and add the launcher mast separately.

## Videos

1. "Kornet-EM live fire" — search term; several demonstration clips (Engineering Technologies, Army forum)
   show the mast raising and missile launch sequence.
2. "Tigr-M Kornet-EM Army forum" — search term for walkaround footage at Russian defence expos.
