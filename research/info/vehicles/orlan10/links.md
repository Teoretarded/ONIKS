# Orlan-10 — drawings and video links

## Drawings / blueprints

- [Army Recognition Orlan-10 data sheet](https://www.armyrecognition.com/russia_russian_missile_system_vehicle_uk/orlan-10_unmanned_aerial_vehicle_uav_drone_technical_data_sheet_specifications_pictures_video.html) — photos, no formal 3-view drawing found.
- ZALA Aero Group product pages (manufacturer) — search "ZALA 421-08 Orlan-10" for current marketing photos.

## Videos

1. "Orlan-10 catapult launch" — search term; several Russian MoD clips of the rail-catapult launch sequence
   (matches `action_catapult.jpg`).
2. "Orlan-10 UAV operation from takeoff to landing" — a Commons-hosted webm exists showing a full sortie
   cycle: search Wikimedia Commons for this exact title.
3. Captured/downed Orlan-10 field-strip videos (Ukrainian sources, 2022-on) — useful for interior/payload-bay
   detail but treat framing/labels in that footage with care (combat context).
