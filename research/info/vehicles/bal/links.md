# 3K60 Bal — drawings and video links

## Drawings / blueprints

- [Military Today Bal-E page](https://www.military-today.com/missiles/bal_e.htm) — profile diagram.
- [Army Recognition Bal-E data sheet](https://www.armyrecognition.com/russia_russian_missile_system_vehicle_uk/bal-e_3k60_ssc-6_sennight_mobile_coastal_defense_missile_system_technical_data_sheet_specifications_pictures.html) — annotated photos.
- MAKS air-show model/maquette photos (see `profile_bal_e_model.jpg`) — useful clean-silhouette reference.

## Videos

1. "Крылатая ракета Бал" / "Bal coastal missile system firing" — search term; Commons hosts at least one
   relevant webm (536th Coastal Missile Artillery Brigade firing, 2021-11-19 — search that title directly on
   Wikimedia Commons).
2. Pacific Fleet training-firing compilations — search "Bal Pacific Fleet training firing" (matches the
   `action_firing_*` photo series' source event).
