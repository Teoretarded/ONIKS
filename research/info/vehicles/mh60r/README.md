# MH-60R Seahawk (Romeo) naval multi-mission helicopter

## Dimensions and mass
- Length (rotors turning, nose to tail rotor tip): 64.8 ft (19.75 m).
- Length (fuselage/stowed, tail folded): ~40.9 ft (12.5 m).
- Rotor diameter: 53.7 ft (16.4 m), 4-blade main rotor.
- Height: 17.2 ft (5.2 m) (tail-rotor pylon up).
- Empty weight: ~15,600 lb (7,076 kg); max gross weight: ~23,500 lb (10,660 kg).
- Speed: max ~180 kt (207 mph, 333 km/h); cruise ~145 kt.
- Crew: 3 (pilot, co-pilot, aircrewman/sensor operator); can carry additional passengers.
Sources: [Wikipedia, Sikorsky SH-60 Seahawk / MH-60R](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sikorsky_SH-60_Seahawk), [Navy fact file, MH-60R](https://www.navy.mil/Resources/Fact-Files/Display-FactFiles/Article/2169908/mh-60rs-seahawk/), [Lockheed Martin/Sikorsky MH-60R page](https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/products/mh-60r-seahawk-helicopter.html).

## Shape and layout (nose to tail)
- **Nose**: chin-mounted APS-153(V) multi-mode radar (AN/APS-147 predecessor also used) in a distinctive bulbous nose radome, sits below/ahead of the cockpit.
- **Cockpit**: two-pilot glass cockpit, stepped nose profile below the windscreen.
- **Cabin**: main cabin behind the cockpit houses the sensor operator's console (Airborne Low Frequency Sonar/ALFS controls, sonobuoy launcher, weapons control), left-side sliding cabin door.
- **Engines**: 2x General Electric T700-GE-401C turboshafts mounted above the cabin roof, exhausts angled aft/down on each side; engine intakes forward-facing above the cabin.
- **Main rotor**: 4-blade articulated rotor on top, blades fold aft for shipboard stowage.
- **Tail boom**: extends aft from the cabin, mounts the AN/ALQ-210 ESM antennas and various blade antennas along its length; tail boom folds (hinges) roughly at mid-length for the shipboard hangar-stowed footprint.
- **Tail rotor**: canted tail rotor at the end of the tail boom (canted a few degrees off-vertical, Sikorsky-typical), horizontal stabilator ahead of it.
- **Under-fuselage**: retractable Airborne Low Frequency Sonar (ALFS) dipping-sonar reel under the cabin (Romeo-specific ASW fit; the MH-60S "Sierra" variant lacks the dipping sonar and radar dome), plus a retractable wheeled undercarriage (fixed tricycle gear, not retractable into the fuselage — wheels always visible).
- **Sponsons/weapons pylons**: stub wings/pylons on each side of the cabin carry AGM-114 Hellfire missiles, Mk 46/54 torpedoes, or external fuel tanks.
- **Sonobuoy launcher**: external rack, aft-lower fuselage, on the starboard side.

## Moving parts
- Main rotor: ~258 rpm (typical Seahawk-family main rotor speed, not officially published per exact variant — treat as approximate).
- Tail rotor spins much faster (geared up from the main rotor transmission, typical multi-thousand rpm range for helicopter tail rotors — no official per-aircraft figure published).
- Main rotor blades and tail boom fold hydraulically/manually for the automated blade-fold system used for shipboard hangar stowage — a multi-step, not-instant sequence.
- ALFS dipping sonar reel extends/retracts on a cable beneath the aircraft while hovering.
- Rescue hoist on some fits, cabin door slides open/closed.

## Markings / colours
- Standard US Navy haze-grey/light-grey overall paint scheme, squadron tail codes and "NAVY" stencil on the tail boom, small national insignia, side number on the nose/tail boom, HSM squadron markings vary by unit.

Note: exact rotor RPM and tail-rotor RPM are not published by the manufacturer in open sources; figures above are the general Seahawk-family approximate values, flagged as such. Radar model (APS-147 vs APS-153) differs by production block.
