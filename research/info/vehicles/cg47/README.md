# CG-47 Ticonderoga-class guided-missile cruiser

## Dimensions and mass
- Length overall: 567 ft (172.8 m).
- Beam: 55 ft (16.8 m).
- Draft: ~34 ft (10.2 m) (sonar dome), ~31 ft hull.
- Displacement: ~9,600–9,800 long tons full load (grew from ~9,407 LT as designed).
- Crew: ~30 officers + ~340 enlisted (~365–400 total depending on era/flag staff).
Sources: [Wikipedia, Ticonderoga-class cruiser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ticonderoga-class_cruiser), [FAS CG-47 page](https://man.fas.org/dod-101/sys/ship/cg-47.htm).

## Speed / propulsion
- 4x General Electric LM2500 gas turbines, 2 shafts, ~80,000–86,000 shp combined (same propulsion plant family as the Spruance-class hull it's built on).
- Max speed: 30+ kt (official "in excess of 30 knots").
Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ticonderoga-class_cruiser).

## Shape and layout (bow to stern)
- **Bow**: Spruance-hull derived, bow sonar dome (SQS-53) below the waterline.
- **Mk 45 5"/54 gun**: single mount, bow, forward of the forward VLS.
- **Forward Mk 26 or Mk 41 VLS**: early hulls (CG-47 to CG-51) carry twin-arm Mk 26 launchers (fwd 2 magazines, 44 cells combined fwd); from CG-52 (Bunker Hill) onward, VLS Mk 41 (61 cells forward).
- **Forward superstructure**: bridge, below the SPY-1A/B radar faces.
- **SPY-1 radar faces**: 4 fixed octagonal phased-array faces mounted on the forward and aft deckhouses (2 pairs, one set forward-facing on the bridge structure, one set aft-facing near the after superstructure) — the defining "double-ended" arrangement unlike the Burke's single deckhouse cluster.
- **Twin stacks**: two closely-spaced funnels amidships, between the forward and aft superstructure blocks (inherited from the Spruance hull).
- **Aft superstructure / mack**: second SPY-1 pair, aft-facing, mounted high.
- **Aft VLS**: matching Mk 26 or Mk 41 (61 cells) aft, ahead of the hangar.
- **Hangar and flight deck**: two-spot helicopter hangar for SH-60/MH-60, RAST recovery, flight deck aft.
- **CIWS**: 2x Phalanx 20mm mounts, one forward (atop/near the bridge structure) and one aft (near the hangar) on most hulls.
- **Harpoon canisters**: 2x quad canister launchers amidships, angled outboard, port and starboard, between the stacks and the aft superstructure (early/Mk26 hulls; some VLS-only late hulls omit them).
- **Stern**: transom stern, twin props/rudders.

## Moving parts
- SPY-1A/B arrays are fixed, no rotation (phased array).
- SPS-49 air-search radar (where fitted) rotates, ~6 rpm class, mounted on the mainmast.
- Mk 26 twin-arm launcher physically swings/trains and reloads from a below-deck magazine between shots (visibly different from VLS's silent hatch-and-launch); Mk 41 VLS has no visible moving launcher, only hatch covers that flip open.
- RAST helicopter hauldown on the flight deck.

## Markings / colours
- Haze grey hull/superstructure, hull number ("CG-##") painted white on the bow, black draft marks, dark grey flight-deck/weatherdeck non-skid.

Note: VLS type (Mk 26 vs Mk 41) and cell count differ by hull within the class — treat "Ticonderoga-class" specs as an early (Mk 26, CG-47/48/49-class) baseline unless the game targets a later Mk 41 hull; both configurations are documented above. Exact draft figures vary slightly between sources.
