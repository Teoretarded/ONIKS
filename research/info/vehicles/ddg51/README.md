# DDG-51 Arleigh Burke-class destroyer, Flight IIA

## Dimensions and mass
- Length overall: 509 ft 6 in (155.3 m) (Flight IIA; earlier Flight I/II are 505 ft/153.9 m).
- Beam: 66 ft (20.1 m).
- Draft: ~30.5 ft (9.3 m) (navigational), ~20.7 ft (6.3 m) hull draft depending on source.
- Height: mast to waterline is not commonly published; hull freeboard forward is tall/flared, superstructure adds several more decks — treat exact overall height as unpublished.
- Displacement: ~9,200 long tons full load (Flight IIA), vs. ~8,300 LT for Flight I.
- Crew: ~280–300 (Flight IIA, with hangar/air det).
Sources: [Wikipedia, Arleigh Burke-class destroyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arleigh_Burke-class_destroyer), [FAS DDG-51 page](https://man.fas.org/dod-101/sys/ship/ddg-51.htm), [Naval Vessel Register](https://www.nvr.navy.mil/).

## Speed / propulsion
- 4x General Electric LM2500-30 gas turbines, 2 shafts, ~100,000 shp (75,000 kW) combined.
- Max speed: 30+ kt (official figure is simply "in excess of 30 knots"; commonly cited ~32.5 kt point estimate is unofficial).
Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arleigh_Burke-class_destroyer).

## Shape and layout (bow to stern)
- **Bow**: sharp knife-edge bow with bulbous/sonar dome below the waterline housing the AN/SQS-53 bow sonar.
- **5"/54 or 5"/62 Mk 45 gun**: single mount on the bow, forward of the superstructure, forward-facing.
- **Forward VLS**: Mk 41 Vertical Launch System, 32 cells, sunk into the deck immediately aft of the gun, ahead of the bridge structure.
- **Bridge/deckhouse**: forward superstructure block with the pilothouse, below the lattice/SPY-1D radar faces.
- **SPY-1D radar faces**: 4 flat octagonal phased-array faces mounted high on the forward deckhouse (2 forward-facing pair visible port/starboard-front, 2 aft-facing on the same structure) giving 360° coverage without rotation — this is the AN/SPY-1D(V) fixed array, not a rotating dish.
- **Mack (mast + stack combined)**: enclosed lattice mast integrated with the forward stack, carries the SPS-67/SPS-73 surface-search radar and various ESM/comms antennas.
- **Aft stack**: second funnel further aft.
- **Aft VLS**: 64-cell Mk 41 VLS block aft of the aft superstructure, forward of the hangar (Flight IIA total 96 cells combined fwd+aft; earlier flights carried 90 with 8 lost when the helicopter hangar was added).
- **Hangar and flight deck (Flight IIA only)**: two-bay helicopter hangar for MH-60R/S, flight deck aft, RAST helicopter recovery system.
- **CIWS/close-in defense**: originally a Phalanx 20mm CIWS aft above the hangar on early hulls; many Flight IIA ships omit it in favor of RAM (SeaRAM) or additional VLS-quad-packed ESSM — mount fit varies hull to hull.
- **Stern**: flush transom, no well deck, twin rudders/props below.

## Moving parts
- SPY-1D arrays are fixed (electronically, not mechanically, scanned) — no rotation.
- SPS-67/SPS-73 surface-search radar on the mack rotates continuously, typical shipboard surface-search rate ~16–24 rpm (not officially published per-hull; general navigational/surface-search radar norm).
- Mk 45 gun train/elevate at classified rates; Mk 41 VLS hatches flip open before each missile launch (~1 second) then close.
- RAST helicopter hauldown system reels the helo into the hangar via a deck-track trolley.

## Markings / colours
- Haze grey hull/superstructure, black draft marks and hull number ("DDG-##") in white on the bow, deck non-skid dark grey on weather decks, red/white day-shapes and small flag markings near the bridge wings.

Note: exact draft and freeboard/height figures vary between Navy fact sheets and secondary sources; treat any single-figure "height" claim as approximate — no official keel-to-mast-top figure is published.
