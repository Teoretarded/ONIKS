# Pantsir-S1 (96K6, SA-22 "Greyhound"), on KamAZ-6560 8x8

Combined gun/missile short-to-medium range air defence system: twin 2A38M 30 mm autocannons + up to 12
57E6/57E6-E missiles, plus its own search/track radar and optical sensor, all on one wheeled chassis.

Deploy time and some mass figures vary by 10-20% between sources.

## Shape and layout

Front to back on the KamAZ-6560 chassis: flat-fronted 3-door cab → open cargo-bed-height chassis deck →
turret ring carrying the full weapon/sensor turret, which rotates independently of the hull. Turret, front to
back: twin 30 mm autocannon barrels (2A38M) low on the turret front, side-by-side; phased-array/slotted search
radar antenna (rectangular panel) on the turret's left side, foldable; missile launch rails — two banks of up
to 6 tubular missile containers each, mounted left and right of the turret centreline, elevating together with
the gun mount; electro-optical sensor head (small turret-mounted ball, thermal + TV + laser) forward-centre
above the guns. No separate erector — the whole turret elevates.

## Moving parts

- **Turret**: full 360° traverse, powered.
- **Gun/missile mount**: elevates from roughly -5° to +82°.
- **Search radar panel**: rotates continuously when in search mode; folds down for travel.
- **Missile banks**: fixed angle relative to gun mount, elevate together; each container is sealed, missiles
  loaded/unloaded as sealed rounds (not reloaded round-by-round in the field quickly).

## Colours / camouflage

Solid olive-drab (parade/most export units) or digital/blotch camouflage (field units, Russian and export
customers vary — see UAE, Syria, and Russian arctic-scheme photos in `images/`).

## Disagreements / gaps

Public engagement-envelope numbers (range, altitude) for the missile and gun vary between manufacturer
(KBP/Instrument Design Bureau) marketing figures and independent estimates; not reproduced here as this file
is geometry/vehicle-focused. Reaction/deploy time of ~5 min is a widely repeated but not independently
verified figure.
