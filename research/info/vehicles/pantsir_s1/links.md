# Pantsir-S1 — drawings and video links

## Drawings / blueprints

- [Military Today Pantsir-S1 page](https://www.military-today.com/artillery/pantsir_s1.htm) — profile diagram.
- [Army Recognition Pantsir-S1 data sheet](https://www.armyrecognition.com/russia_russian_missile_system_vehicle_uk/pantsir-s1_96k6_9k96_greyhound_sa-22_tracked_wheeled_self-propelled_air_defense_missile_gun_system_technical_data_sheet_specifications_pictures.html) — annotated photos.
- Zvezda/Trumpeter 1/35 and 1/72 Pantsir-S1 scale model kits — good box-art profile/plan references (unfree).

## Videos

1. "Pantsir-S1 live fire" — search term; multiple Russian MoD and export-customer (UAE, Serbia) live-fire
   compilations showing turret elevation and missile launch.
2. MAKS/Engineering Technologies air-show walkarounds — search "Pantsir-S1 MAKS airshow" for close, static
   turret detail with the radar panel deployed.
3. "Pantsir-S1 radar deployment" — shorter clips showing the search-radar panel folding/unfolding.
