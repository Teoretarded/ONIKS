# S-400 (5P85SM2-01 launcher + 92N6E radar) — drawings and video links

## Drawings / blueprints

- [Military Today S-400 page](https://www.military-today.com/missiles/s400.htm) — profile diagrams for launcher and radar.
- [Army Recognition S-400 data sheet](https://www.armyrecognition.com/russia_russian_missile_system_vehicle_uk/s-400_triumf_sa-21_growler_mobile_air_defense_missile_system_technical_data_sheet_specifications_pictures.html) — annotated photos of the full battalion set (launcher, 92N6E, 96L6E, 55K6E).
- [FAS S-400 page](https://man.fas.org/dod-101/sys/missile/row/s-400.htm) — background diagram.

## Videos

1. "S-400 Triumf deployment" — search term; several Russian MoD clips show launcher erection and 92N6E
   antenna raising during exercises.
2. Victory Day parade drive-past footage (Moscow, various years) — good for convoy spacing and chassis
   proportions of both launcher and radar vehicles together.
3. "S-400 missile launch test" — search term for actual firing footage (erector at full elevation, canister
   lid opening).
