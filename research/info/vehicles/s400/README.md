# S-400 Triumf (SA-21 "Growler"): 5P85SM2-01/5P85TE2 launcher + 92N6E "Grave Stone" radar

Long-range/high-altitude SAM system. Launcher vehicles (semi-trailer or self-propelled, 4-8 tubes depending on
variant) are cued by the 92N6E multifunction engagement radar; a battalion also has a 55K6E command post and,
optionally, 96L6E acquisition radar and 91N6E "Big Bird" long-range surveillance radar.

## Shape and layout (self-propelled launcher, 5P85SM2-01)

Front to back: BAZ-64022 flat-fronted cab (rounded corners, small windows) → open chassis frame → 4 large
tubular launch canisters carried in a single row, mounted on a hydraulic erector frame at the rear of the
chassis. Canisters lie flat/near-horizontal for travel, pivot up to near-vertical (~90°) on the erector arm
to fire, one canister opening upward like a hinged lid before missile launch. No separate stabiliser jacks
visible in most photos (self-propelled variant fires from wheels).

92N6E radar: box-shaped equipment cabin on a similar heavy 8x8/semi-trailer chassis, with the large flat
phased-array antenna face mounted on a rotating turntable at the rear — antenna folds down flat over the cabin
roof for travel and rises/rotates to vertical, facing the sky, when deployed.

## Moving parts

- **Launcher erector**: 4-canister rack pivots from horizontal to vertical for launch.
- **92N6E antenna**: folds flat for travel, erects and continuously rotates in search/track mode.
- **96L6E acquisition radar** (companion system, own vehicle): rotating antenna on a telescoping mast, raised
  well above the vehicle for over-the-horizon search — not the focus of this note but often seen alongside
  S-400 batteries in photos.

## Colours / camouflage

Solid olive-drab most common on parade and exercise photos; digital/blotch camouflage seen on some deployed
units.

## Disagreements / gaps

Mass, range and deploy-time figures vary across the several S-400 launcher variants and are not consistently
distinguished in open sources — the self-propelled 5P85SM2-01 figures above are best-estimate. No detailed
public technical drawing of the 92N6E turntable/antenna dimensions was found; photo-derived only.
