# K-300P Bastion-P — drawings and video links

## Drawings / blueprints

- [Military Today Bastion-P page](https://www.military-today.com/missiles/bastion_p.htm) — profile diagram.
- [Army Recognition Bastion-P data sheet](https://www.armyrecognition.com/russia_russian_missile_system_vehicle_uk/bastion-p_3k55_k300p_mobile_coastal_defence_missile_system_technical_data_sheet_specifications_pictures_video.html) — annotated photos, no formal line drawing found.
- Scale-model box art/plans: search "Bastion K-300P 1/72 model" (Zvezda and Trumpeter kits exist) — useful silhouette reference, unfree.

## Videos

1. "Береговой ракетный комплекс Бастион" (Russian MoD compilation) — search this title on YouTube; shows TEL erection and launch sequence.
2. "K-300P Bastion coastal missile system firing" — search term; several exercise clips from Black Sea Fleet drills.
3. Victory Day parade drive-past footage (Moscow/Rostov-on-Don, various years) — good for chassis proportions and convoy spacing.
