# K-300P Bastion-P (SS-C-5 "Stooge"), 3K55 coastal missile system

Three vehicles on the MZKT-7930 "Astrolog" 8x8 chassis, sharing one hull: K340P transporter-erector-launcher
(TEL, carries 2 x P-800 Oniks/Yakhont), K380R command-and-control vehicle, K342P transloader (crane + spare
rounds). A battery is typically 4 TEL + 1-2 command vehicles + transloaders + support.

## Dimensions and mass (MZKT-7930 chassis, shared by all three variants)

| | |
|---|---|
| Length | ~11.2 m |
| Width | ~3.1 m |
| Height | ~3.0 m (cab roof; TEL with canisters stowed is taller) |
| Wheelbase/axles | 4 axles (8x8), axle spacing ~1.8+2.0+1.4 m (approx, not officially published) |
| Combat mass (TEL, loaded) | ~41 t |
| Road speed | up to 60 km/h |
| Range | ~1,000 km (chassis fuel range) |
| Crew | TEL: 3; K380R command vehicle: crew varies, ~5 |
| Deploy time | ~5 min from march to ready-to-fire (commonly cited, not officially confirmed) |

Sources: [Wikipedia: K-300P Bastion-P](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-300P_Bastion-P), [Military Today: Bastion-P](https://www.military-today.com/missiles/bastion_p.htm), [Army Recognition Bastion-P data sheet](https://www.armyrecognition.com/russia_russian_missile_system_vehicle_uk/bastion-p_3k55_k300p_mobile_coastal_defence_missile_system_technical_data_sheet_specifications_pictures_video.html). Figures vary between sources by up to 10%; treat as best-estimate, not official.

## Shape and layout (K340P TEL)

Front to back: MZKT-7930 cab (flat-fronted, 2-3 doors per side, small windows, camouflage-netted roof rack) →
short exposed chassis/engine deck section → hydraulic erector arm pivot (rear third of the hull) → two long
cylindrical transport-launch canisters (TLCs) carried side by side, each ~8.9 m long, ~0.7 m diameter, angled
slightly outward → 4 stabiliser/outrigger jacks (one per side, front and rear of the load area) that deploy
before erection. No visible turret; canisters lie flat over the rear chassis when travelling, then the whole
pack pivots up to ~90° (near-vertical) on the erector arm for launch.

K380R command vehicle: same MZKT-7930 cab, but the rear bed carries a closed van-body module (radio/data
antennas folded on the roof, no canisters). K342P transloader: MZKT-7930 tractor pulling/carrying a crane
(boom folded along the vehicle when travelling) plus reserve TLCs on a support cradle, used to reload TELs
in the field without returning to depot.

## Moving parts

- **Erector**: canister pack swings from horizontal (travel) to near-vertical for launch; cited erection time
  ~5 minutes including outrigger deployment (unofficial, commonly repeated figure).
- **Outriggers**: 4 hydraulic jacks lower to stabilise the chassis before erection; deploy in well under a
  minute per available footage.
- **Transloader crane**: boom unfolds and swings to lift a TLC from the transloader's cradle onto a TEL;
  a multi-minute process per round, not publicly timed.

## Colours / camouflage

Standard Russian MoD olive-drab/green solid colour on parade vehicles; digital or blotch woodland/arctic
camouflage patterns seen in field photos (see `images/action_*`). TLCs are matte pale green/grey.

## Disagreements / gaps

Deploy-to-fire and erection timings are widely repeated (~5 min) but not sourced to an official document —
treat as approximate. No public axle-spacing dimensions found; estimated from chassis photos.
