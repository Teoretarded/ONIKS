# Monolit-B (Monolith-B / Mineral-ME family), coastal over-the-horizon targeting radar

Passive/active target-designation radar complex for coastal-defence missile systems (Bastion, Bal); detects
and tracks surface ships and hands off targeting data to the missile batteries. Mounted on an 8x8 MZKT chassis
(same family as Bastion/Bal support vehicles).

## Shape and layout

Front to back: MZKT 8x8 cab (flat-fronted, similar to Bastion/Bal chassis) → chassis deck carrying an
equipment/electronics cabin (closed box body) → large flat-panel or dish radar antenna mounted on a
foldable/erectable mast at the rear, laid flat along the roof when travelling and raised near-vertical when
deployed. No canisters or missiles — this is a sensor vehicle only. Support antennas (smaller whip/log-periodic
types) on the cab roof and cabin sides.

## Moving parts

- **Antenna mast/dish**: folds flat for travel, erects for search/track operation; exact raise time not public.
- No turret, no weapon components.

## Colours / camouflage

Same olive-drab or camouflage-pattern finish as other MZKT-family coastal-defence vehicles (see `images/`).

## Disagreements / gaps

Very little public technical data exists for Monolit-B specifically (as opposed to the related Mineral-ME
shipborne/coastal system); most cited figures are estimates from the shared chassis, not confirmed
system specs. No official deploy-time or detection-range figures found in open sources.
