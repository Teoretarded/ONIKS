# AIM-120 AMRAAM — video and photo links

## Videos

1. **AIM-120 AMRAAM Air-To-Air Missile Live Launch from F/A-18 Super Hornet** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h3vH-TKsWMA — VFA-41 Black Aces live-fire; watch the rail-drop-to-ignition moment and how fast the missile outruns the jet.
2. **AIM-120 AMRAAM Missile Firing From F-15s** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5VkZ4Hr8tSA — multiple releases, good repeat look at ignition flash and thin smoke trail from a chase view.
3. **F-35B Lightning II Fires AIM-120 AMRAAM Missile For The First Time During WTI Training** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b0lcla1NtK8 — internal-bay-style release into open air, useful for ejector-launch (non-rail) separation behavior.
4. **Raytheon AMRAAM promotional/test footage** (search "AMRAAM F3R separation test Super Hornet Raytheon") — manufacturer cut of an F/A-18F Super Hornet AIM-120D-3/F3R launch at Point Mugu Sea Range; clean daylight shot of rail separation and motor light.
