## Note
Most available public footage is wartime phone/drone video focused on the impact, not a clean side-on shot of
the soft-launch-then-ignite sequence at the tube; treat the eject/ignition beat in README.md as inferred from
general soft-launch ATGM behaviour rather than a Kornet-specific measured clip.
