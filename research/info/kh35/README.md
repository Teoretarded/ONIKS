# Kh-35U / Kh-35 Uran (3K60 Bal coastal system)

## Specs
- Length: 3.85 m (air-launched) to 4.4 m (surface/coastal, includes booster). Diameter: 0.42 m. Wingspan:
  1.33 m. Mass: 520–670 kg depending on variant.
  [Kh-35 — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kh-35)
- Subsonic: Mach 0.8–0.85 (~980–1,041 km/h), powered by an R95TP-300 turbofan (360 kgf thrust, kerosene) once
  cruising. Surface/coastal launches add a solid rocket booster for the initial boost phase (turbofans can't
  self-launch from a standstill). [Wikipedia]
- Flight profile: ~10–15 m AGL en route, dropping to ~4 m at the terminal/attack phase — a classic low
  sea-skimmer profile, less dramatic in altitude change than Oniks/Kalibr's supersonic terminal dash.
- Bal system: 4 self-propelled TELs (MZKT-7930 8x8), each carrying up to 8 containerised missiles; launchers can
  sit up to 10 km inland from the coast. [Wikipedia], [3K60 Bal — Weaponsystems.net](https://weaponsystems.net/system/140-3K60+Bal)

## What a launch looks like
1. **Container**: missile ships in a sealed box/tube canister on the TEL, one of up to 8 per vehicle — visually
   a plain rectangular box, no visible missile until launch.
2. **Boost**: solid rocket booster fires first (needed since the turbofan sustainer can't produce static
   thrust) — expect a short, bright, smoky boost phase similar in character to other coastal TEL launches
   (Oniks/Bastion), just from a smaller missile/booster.
3. **Booster separation and turbofan light**: booster burns out and drops away, the turbofan lights for cruise
   — no public numbers on booster burn time or drop-off visuals specific to Kh-35.
4. **Level-off**: missile settles into low-level subsonic cruise (~10–15 m) — much slower and lower than
   Oniks/Kalibr, more like a Harpoon/Exocet in feel (Wikipedia notes it's roughly comparable in performance to
   those).
5. **Terminal**: descends further to ~4 m for the final approach — a smaller altitude change than Oniks/Kalibr
   since it started low already.
6. **TEL launch angle**: reference photos (`images/bal_tel_training_firing_*.jpg`) show the launch containers
   raised to a steep incline off the truck bed, similar to Bastion.

**Gaps**: no sourced booster burn time, exit speed, or exact pitch-over/level-off timing for Kh-35 specifically.
