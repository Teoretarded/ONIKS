# Launch reference

Real-world reference for how the game's rounds launch and fly. One folder per round: `README.md` (specs, launch
timeline, sources), `links.md` (best videos with timestamps, photo pages, licences), `images/` (free-licence stills).

| Folder | Round | Launcher | Launch in one line |
|---|---|---|---|
| sm6, sm2 | RIM-174 SM-6, RIM-66 SM-2 | Mk 41 VLS | hot launch, ~2-4 s vertical, gentle lean; Mk 72 (~6 s) drops, smaller Mk 104 plume |
| essm | RIM-162 ESSM | Mk 41 quad-pack | turns almost at once off the cell |
| tomahawk | RGM-109 | Mk 41 VLS | hard tip-over to level within seconds; booster drops, wings out, near-invisible cruise |
| ram | RIM-116 | Mk 49 box | near-horizontal, short bright flame |
| mk45 | 5"/62 round | Mk 45 gun | single sharp flash, smoke puff, recoil every ~3 s |
| phalanx_cram | 20 mm | Phalanx / C-RAM | naval AP rounds show little tracer; C-RAM tracers form a solid rope, self-destruct sparkles |
| aim120 | AIM-120 | F/A-18E rail | thin smoke trail, pulls ahead of the jet in ~1 s |
| slam_er | AGM-84H | F/A-18E drop | free-fall, fins open, turbojet start without a flash |
| hellfire | AGM-114 | MH-60R rail | thin trail, outruns the helicopter at once |
| oniks | 3M55 | Bastion TEL / VLS | booster inside the ramjet; high or low cruise, ~5-15 m at the end |
| kalibr | 3M-54 | 3S14 VLS | cold eject then ignition; subsonic cruise, supersonic final stage |
| kh35 | Kh-35U | Bal launcher | booster from the container, low sea-skim |
| s400_48n6 | 48N6E3 | S-400 TEL | cold launch ~30 m up, ignition in the air, sharp tip-over |
| pantsir_57e6 | 57E6 + 2A38 | Pantsir-S1 | cold eject, 2 s booster to ~Mach 3.8; twin 30 mm near-solid tracer stream |
| kornet | 9M133 | tripod / vehicle | quiet eject puff, motor lights a few metres out |

Where footage doesn't show something (exact burn times, pitch-over timing for Oniks/Kh-35/Kornet), the notes say so.
Treat those as the animator's call, not fact.
