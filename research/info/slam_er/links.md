# AGM-84H/K SLAM-ER — video and photo links

## Videos

1. **CENTCOM-released F/A-18E SLAM-ER launch from USS Abraham Lincoln** (July 2026) — search "F/A-18E Super Hornet AGM-84K SLAM-ER launch Abraham Lincoln CENTCOM" — operational strike footage, watch for the drop-then-light sequence; covered by [Army Recognition](https://www.armyrecognition.com/news/navy-news/2026/u-s-navy-f-a-18e-super-hornet-carrying-an-agm-84k-reveals-uss-abraham-lincolns-standoff-strike-posture).
2. **DCS F/A-18C Tutorial 21 – SLAM-ER Missile** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XQC5wuQGWYc — simulation, NOT real footage; only useful as a rough shape/deployment reference, do not treat as visually authoritative.
3. Search "SLAM-ER separation test NAS Patuxent River" and "AGM-84 SLAM captive carry drop test" on YouTube/NAVAIR channels for older AGM-84E SLAM drop-test footage (SLAM-ER shares the airframe/release mechanics).

## Photos (Wikimedia Commons, public domain US DoD)

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:F-18C_with_SLAM-ER_missile_and_AWW-13_pods_in_flight.jpg — captive carriage, wings/fins folded, good reference for pre-launch shape.
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:AGM-84_Harpoon_(SLAM).jpg — earlier AGM-84E SLAM (not SLAM-ER; nose/seeker differs) but same body/wing/fin deployment geometry — useful shape reference only.
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:AGM-84_Harpoon — browse for more loading/carriage photos across the family.
