# AGM-114 Hellfire — video and photo links

## Videos

1. **MH-60 seahawk launching hellfire missile** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYeu4w2NH_U — direct MH-60 rail launch, watch ignition flash and how quickly the missile clears the rail.
2. **Hellfire II Air-to-Surface Missile Launch from MH-60 Seahawk** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-M7ZI5Xs79Y — clean daylight launch, good plume-thinness reference.
3. **Hellfire II Air-to-Surface Missile Launch from MH-60 Seahawk** (alt upload) — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eif0EtC-zBY — same event/type, different edit/quality, useful second angle.
4. **US Navy MH-60 fires missiles into ship trying to reach Iran** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIiF884UTC4 — recent (2025/2026) operational footage, real-world target engagement rather than a range shoot.
5. **Navy Hellfire missile ultra-slow-motion launch (LCS/ship-based, not MH-60)** — see [Inverse coverage](https://www.inverse.com/article/28872-us-navy-lcs-uss-detroit-hellfire-missile-slow-motion-launch) — useful for frame-by-frame ignition-flash detail even though the platform differs.
