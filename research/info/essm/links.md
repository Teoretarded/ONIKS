# ESSM — video and photo links

## Videos
- [DVIDS: search "ESSM launch"](https://www.dvidshub.net/search/?q=ESSM+launch) — browse for the clearest quad-pack launch clip; no single best video surfaced reliably in text search, check current listings.
- [MK 41 Vertical Launching System (VLS) — Military.com](https://www.military.com/video/logistics-and-supplies/naval-equipment/mk-41-vertical-launching-system-vls/3204426079001) — general Mk 41 hot-launch B-roll, applicable to ESSM's shared hatch/exit behavior.

## Photo pages
- [Wikimedia Commons: File:RIM-162 launched from USS Carl Vinson (CVN-70) July 2010.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:RIM-162_launched_from_USS_Carl_Vinson_(CVN-70)_July_2010.jpg) — source of the image in images/; clear launch with visible plume and the fast turn onto course.
- [Wikimedia Commons: Category:RIM-162 Evolved Sea Sparrow](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:RIM-162_Evolved_Sea_Sparrow) — mostly loading/handling photos, but check for new launch stills over time.
- [Missile Threat / CSIS: Evolved Seasparrow Missile (ESSM)](https://missilethreat.csis.org/defsys/evolved-seasparrow-missile-essm/) — spec page with diagrams.
- [Defense Industry Daily: RIM-162 ESSM Missile — Naval Anti-Air in a Quad Pack](https://www.defenseindustrydaily.com/rim-162-essm-missile-naval-anti-air-in-a-quad-pack-03924/) — background on the quad-pack canister.

Note: public-domain ESSM launch photography is thinner than for SM-2/SM-6/Tomahawk — only one clean Commons launch photo was found; DVIDS is the better source for more, search directly by ship name if a specific launch is needed.
