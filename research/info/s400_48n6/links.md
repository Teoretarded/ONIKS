# S-400 / 48N6 — links

## Video
- [S-400 Triumf Missile System In Action — YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebVXwUl0azI) — live-fire exercise footage; watch for the cold-launch pop-up-then-ignite pattern described in README.md.
- [Russian S-400 Triumf Air Defence System In Action — YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kE394GDiN4A) — another live-fire compilation.
- [Targets destroyed: Spectacular VIDEO shows Russia's S-400 air defense system in action — RT](https://www.rt.com/russia/471474-s400-air-defense-russia/) — article with Kapustin Yar exercise footage (8/8 targets), multiple cold launches visible.
- [Why S-400 fires its missiles straight up into the air — WION](https://www.wionews.com/photos/why-the-s-400-fires-its-missiles-straight-up-into-the-air-1784402356550) — explainer with stills of the vertical cold-launch pop-up.

## Photos
- [Wikimedia Commons: Category:S-400 Triumf](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:S-400_Triumf) — TEL/canister photos, used in `images/`.
- `images/s300_family_naval_cold_launch_varyag.jpg` is an S-300F (naval, 5V55/48N6 missile family) cold-launch
  photo — same cold-launch mechanism and missile family lineage as the land-based S-400's 48N6E3, useful for the
  gas-eject-then-ignite look even though it's a different hull.

still above is the best free-licensed stand-in for that look. See the Video section above for footage that shows
the real sequence but isn't downloadable/free.
