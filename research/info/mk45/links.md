# Mk 45 5"/62 gun — video and photo links

## Videos
- [DVIDS: USS Dewey Fires 5-inch Gun](https://www.dvidshub.net/video/836211/uss-dewey-fires-5-inch-gun) — USS Dewey (DDG 105), March 2022, clean live-fire exercise footage.
- [Guided-Missile Destroyer MK 45 5-Inch Gun Live-Fire — YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uCvPViECgl0) — multiple shots, good for the recoil/rhythm of rapid fire.
- [5-inch 62-caliber Mk 45 Naval Gun Live Fire – Arleigh Burke-class Destroyer — YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AbkG9k3Q2Dk) — specifically the 62-cal Mod 4, matches the current in-service variant.
- [US Navy 5-Inch Mk 45 Naval Gun LIVE FIRE — YouTube Shorts](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/apxQTXMOXeI) — short clip, close framing on the muzzle flash.
