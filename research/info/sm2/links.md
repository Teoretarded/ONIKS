# SM-2 — video and photo links

## Videos
- [DVIDS: USS Higgins Live Fire Missile Launch B-Roll](https://www.dvidshub.net/video/919106/uss-higgins-live-fire-missile-launch-b-roll) — clean deck-level shot of an SM-2 leaving the forward VLS, good for the vertical-rise and smoke-column timing.
- [DVIDS: USS Shoup Launches SM-2 Missile During Live Fire With a Purpose Exercise](https://www.dvidshub.net/video/857966/uss-shoup-launches-sm-2-missile-during-live-fire-with-purpose-exercise) — SM-2 Block IIIA, daylight, full launch-to-climb.
- [DVIDS: USS Rafael Peralta fires an SM-2 standard missile](https://www.dvidshub.net/video/891243/uss-rafael-peralta-fires-sm-2-standard-missile) — another clean daylight launch angle.
- [DVIDS: USS Zumwalt (DDG 1000) Live-Fire Test of SM-2 Missile](https://www.dvidshub.net/video/770097/uss-zumwalt-ddg-1000-live-fire-test-sm-2-missile) — Mk 57 VLS (not Mk 41) but same hot-launch behavior, useful comparison.
- [DVIDS: SM2 Launch](https://www.dvidshub.net/video/476733/sm2-launch) — generic title, worth checking for a different ship/angle.
