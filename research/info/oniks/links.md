# Oniks / Bastion — links

## Video
- [P-800 Oniks Anti-Ship Missile Launched from Coastal Battery! (YouTube Shorts)](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/D1RPkBB30zw) — short clip, launch fills almost the whole short; best single clean shot of the TEL firing.
- [The Oniks Missile (YouTube Shorts)](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/LvKXqVEAC-E) — second short, different angle/compilation.
- [Russia Unleashes Unstoppable Ship Killer Monster Missile Oniks — WION Top Stories](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iXMbZE_B_rA) — longer news segment, includes MoD launch b-roll plus talking-head; skip to the b-roll for footage.
- [Russia MoD launches supersonic anti-ship "Oniks P-800" cruise missile — Army Recognition](https://armyrecognition.com/news/navy-news/2019/russia-mod-launches-supersonic-anti-ship-oniks-p-800-cruise-missile) — article embedding official MoD launch footage from Chukotka, first Bastion firing there.

`launch_tel_model_canisters_raised_armia2018.jpg`, `static_tel_model_launcher_bed_open_armia2018.jpg`) are

No clearly free-licensed **flight** images (missile actually airborne, with plume/smoke trail) were found on
Commons — real launches are covered only by MoD video clips (see Video section above), which are not stills we
can download under a confirmed free licence. Gap: `flight_*` is not covered by any image in this folder; use
the linked videos for plume/trail look by eye.

## Note
No public frame-by-frame breakdown of Oniks booster separation or cover jettison was found — the videos above
are the best available for judging the plume, boost duration and climb angle by eye.
