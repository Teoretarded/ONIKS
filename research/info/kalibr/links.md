## Gaps
- No true sequential launch-frame set (hatch opening → cold eject → ignition → pitch-over) was found under a
  clear free licence; the two pre-existing launch photos (submarine and ship-deck) are single-moment shots, not
  a sequence. Note above (3S14 cold-launch closeup) still stands.
- Only one clean in-flight/cruise shot found (`flight_submarine_cruise_trail.jpg`); no closeup cruise-altitude
  or terminal-sprint imagery under a free licence.
