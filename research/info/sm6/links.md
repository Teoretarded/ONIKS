# SM-6 — video and photo links

- [HMAS Sydney fires SM-6 (first RAN launch)](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=HMAS+Sydney+SM-6+launch) — Australian DoD release, Aug 2024; watch the vertical exit and the smoke column against open sky. Referenced in [The Aviationist](https://theaviationist.com/2024/08/13/royal-australian-navy-fires-sm-6/) and [Defence Security Asia](https://defencesecurityasia.com/en/australian-destroyer-hmas-sydney-launches-sm-6-missile-for-the-first-time/).
- [USS Preble fires SM-6 Dual II](https://www.dvidshub.net/search/?q=USS+Preble+SM-6) — DVIDS, search "Preble SM-6"; shows launch-to-intercept test framing.
- [MK 41 Vertical Launching System (VLS) — Military.com](https://www.military.com/video/logistics-and-supplies/naval-equipment/mk-41-vertical-launching-system-vls/3204426079001) — general Mk 41 launch B-roll, useful for the hatch/hot-launch look shared by all Mk 41 rounds.
- [MK 41 Vertical Launching System (VLS) — YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z2KSqSvKz-Y) — same system, multiple launch angles.
