# Phalanx CIWS / C-RAM — video and photo links

## Videos

1. **US Navy Phalanx CIWS gun in action firing (best collection of live fire Phalanx footage)** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V5DU-uoLwj4 — compilation of real Navy live-fire clips, day and night, multiple ships.
2. **Awesome Phalanx CIWS in action at night, shooting down incoming rockets in Afghanistan** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CbEo-P9iJUE — real land-based C-RAM engagement footage, night tracer stream and self-destruct sparkle clearly visible.
3. **Phalanx CIWS C-RAM in action at night compilation** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BnrSTkidXa8 — mostly Baghdad-area real intercepts, good repeated look at the tracer "rope" and burst cadence.
4. **NIGHT LIVE FIRE EXERCISE OF ANTI AIRCRAFT GUN PHALANX CIWS** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JDOKMj0DUSc — Navy night live-fire exercise, clean single-mount tracer stream.
5. **Phalanx CIWS Close In Weapons System • Live Fire Test** — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-L0ZAGOuaqg — daylight test firing, shows loading and the muzzle-flash/haze look without tracers (naval AP ammo).

Avoid ArmA/milsim-tagged results ("C-RAM PAC Fire at Night - Phalanx CIWS - ... Simulation", "150 Phalanx CIWS fire at same time") — those are game footage, not real weapon behaviour, and should not be used as visual reference.
