# Pantsir-S1 / 57E6 — links

## Video
- [Pantsir-S SA-22 Greyhound firing in action ground-to-air missile — Russian army, RIA Novosti — YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rLiv0TXVQ44) — missile launches from the tube array, good for boost-phase plume.
- [Pantsir-S1 Air defence missile/gun system — YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=50TmAd6AxfE) — combined missile + 2A38M gun demonstration.
- [Russian Pantsir-S1 In Action — YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=udm93uITPgI) — more recent (2025) footage.
- [Russian Pantsir Missile System Appears to Malfunction in Fiery Video — Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/russian-pantsir-missile-system-error-misfire-friendly-fire-malfunction-viral-video-1747398) — shows three clean tube launches before the well-documented misfire; useful for the boost/plume look even though the article's focus is the accident, not for target/guidance behaviour.

## Photos
- [Wikimedia Commons: Category:Pantsir-S1](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Pantsir-S1) — turret, missile tubes and 2A38M guns, used in `images/`.
