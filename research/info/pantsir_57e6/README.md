# 57E6 (Pantsir-S1) + 2A38M guns

## Missile specs
- Length: 3.16 m. Diameter: 90 mm (booster stage) / 76 mm (sustainer stage) — the booster is a wider sleeve
  around/ahead of the thinner sustainer. Mass: 94 kg sealed-in-container / 76 kg at launch.
  [Pantsir-S1 — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pantsir-S1)
- Two-stage solid-fuel: first-stage booster accelerates hard for the first ~2 seconds of flight, then separates
  from the sustainer stage. Peak speed 1,300 m/s (Mach 3.8) at booster separation, dropping to 780 m/s
  (Mach 2.3) by 18 km range. [Wikipedia]
- Guidance: radio/optical command from the vehicle, up to 4 missiles guided in flight simultaneously (visual
  cue: missiles fired in quick succession rather than one-at-a-time-then-wait).

## 2A38M twin autocannon
- Calibre: 30 mm. Rate of fire: up to 2,500 rounds/min per gun (so ~5,000 rpm combined, twin-barrel mount).
  Muzzle velocity: 960 m/s. [Wikipedia]
- Visual: twin barrels fire near-simultaneously in short bursts; at that rate of fire the tracers (every few
  rounds, standard AAA practice) read almost as a solid line/stream rather than distinct flashes, with a sharp
  buzzing/ripping report rather than individual cracks.

## What a launch looks like
1. **Cold launch from tube**: 57E6 rounds sit sealed in transport-launch tubes on the turret (12 tubes typical
   Pantsir-S1 loadout); each round is ejected from its tube before the booster ignites — the same cold-launch
   family behaviour as S-400/Kalibr, scaled down.
2. **Boost**: booster ignites just clear of the tube, burns for ~2 s, accelerating the missile to its peak
   speed (Mach 3.8) very quickly — visually a short, intense, bright launch flash and plume compared to the
   longer boost phases of the bigger naval/coastal missiles.
3. **Booster separation**: booster drops away at ~2 s, sustainer continues — expect a visible discrete flash/
   puff and a piece separating, more abrupt than a slow burnout fade.
4. **Cruise/intercept**: sustainer carries the missile out to engage, decelerating from Mach 3.8 to ~Mach 2.3 by
   18 km as the fuel burns down and drag dominates.
5. **Salvo behaviour**: because guidance is multiplexed for up to 4 missiles, expect back-to-back launches from
   different tubes within a few seconds of each other during an engagement, not a single missile and a long
   pause.
6. **Guns**: 2A38M twin 30 mm fire in short high-rate bursts for close/cheap targets — bright rapid muzzle flash
   at the turret, dense tracer stream, brief (sub-second to ~1 s) bursts typical for AAA engagements.
