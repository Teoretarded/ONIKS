~170x140 km coast). Public, encyclopaedic facts only — game-catalogue framing, no tactics or weapon internals.

Ten folders below carry full detail (`README.md` + `images/`); the rest are catalogued here only, pending a
future pass.

## Top-10 shortlist

1. **Zircon (3M22)** — the hypersonic round the coastal side is missing; raises the bar on intercept timing and
   forces a visible reaction from Aegis/SM-6.
2. **NSM (Naval Strike Missile)** — a low-signature US/coalition anti-ship round that reads differently on
   screen from SLAM-ER/Tomahawk, and arms ships, aircraft and a truck launcher (NMESIS) at once.
3. **LRASM** — the carrier air wing's long-range anti-ship punch; distinct silhouette and profile from AIM-120D.
4. **Admiral Gorshkov (Project 22350)** — the coastal side's answer to Ticonderoga/Burke: a real multi-role
   frigate carrying Zircon/Kalibr/Poliment-Redut.
5. **Constellation (FFG-62)** — closes the escort gap in the carrier group roster with a currently-fielding
   US frigate design.
6. **Yasen-M (Severodvinsk)** — a second, more modern Russian sub to contrast with Kilo and pressure Virginia.
7. **Su-57 (Felon)** — the coastal side's 5th-gen counter to F-35C; visually and mechanically distinct silhouette.
8. **F-35C (Lightning II)** — completes the carrier air wing alongside F/A-18E/EA-18G with a stealth ingress role.
9. **Tor-M2** — cheap, mobile short-range air defence that protects TELs and troops from drones/aircraft, a gap
   in the current roster (S-400/Pantsir cover the far and near tiers but not this point-defence niche).
10. **SPY-6 / Aegis Combat System** — a sensor/C2 addition rather than a weapon: shows the fleet's shared radar
    and engagement picture, the thing that makes SM-6/ESSM coordinated defence make sense on screen.
