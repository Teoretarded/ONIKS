# 3M22 Zircon (Tsirkon)

Role: Russian ship/submarine-launched hypersonic anti-ship and land-attack cruise missile. Fired from the
universal 3S14 vertical launch cells already used by Kalibr and Oniks, so it drops into the existing Bastion/
Kalibr launcher family rather than needing new hardware.

## Specs

- Length: reported ~8-10 m (canister-launched, exact hull dimensions not officially published) —
  [Missile Threat / CSIS](https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/3m22-tsirkon/)
- Diameter: ~0.6 m at the 3S14 cell interface — [Naval News](https://www.navalnews.com/naval-news/2022/05/russia-conducts-first-tsirkon-hypersonic-missile-launch-from-a-submarine/)
- Launch mass: estimated ~3,000-4,000 kg (unconfirmed by official source)
- Speed: (sources differ: Mach 8-9 peak per CSIS / Mach 6.8 sustained cruise per Wikipedia's compiled figures) —
  [Missile Threat / CSIS](https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/3m22-tsirkon/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3M22_Zircon)
- Range: (sources differ: official Russian figure >1,000 km / Western estimates ~400-450 km, up to ~740 km on a
  semi-ballistic profile) — [TASS, Dec 2019](https://tass.com/defense/1096863), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3M22_Zircon)
- First operational firing: from frigate *Admiral Gorshkov*, 2022-2023 test series —
  [Naval News](https://www.navalnews.com/naval-news/2023/02/russia-officially-inducts-zircon-hypersonic-missile/)

## Visible parts / what it looks like in action

- Cold-launched vertically from a ship's 3S14 deck hatch (a square flush cover flips open), a brief gas-ejection
  puff before the booster ignites — matches the existing Kalibr/Oniks launch VFX language.
- Booster burns for a few seconds with a bright, dense exhaust plume, then the missile noses over onto a
  climbing, near-vertical-to-shallow trajectory before leveling into cruise.
- No public imagery yet shows the missile in level hypersonic cruise or its terminal approach; released photos
  are all launch-moment shots from the deck of *Admiral Gorshkov*.
- Airframe silhouette (from official release stills) is a slender cylindrical body with a pointed ogive nose and
  small cruciform fins near the tail, consistent with the shared 3S14-family canister diameter.
