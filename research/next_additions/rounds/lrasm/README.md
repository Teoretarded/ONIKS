# AGM-158C LRASM (Long Range Anti-Ship Missile)

Role: Lockheed Martin stealthy, long-range, autonomously-guided anti-ship missile derived from the JASSM-ER
airframe. Carried by carrier air wing strike aircraft (F/A-18E/F, F-35C) and by land-based B-1B bombers — the
carrier group's answer to Zircon/Oniks/Kh-35U in the coastal-side roster.

## Specs

- Length: 4.4 m — [US Navy fact file, NAVAIR](https://www.navair.navy.mil/)
- Weight: ~1,111 kg — [US Navy fact file, NAVAIR](https://www.navair.navy.mil/)
- Speed: high subsonic — [Lockheed Martin LRASM page](https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/products/long-range-anti-ship-missile-lrasm.html)
- Range: 200+ nautical miles (~370+ km) — [Lockheed Martin LRASM page](https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/products/long-range-anti-ship-missile-lrasm.html)
- Warhead: penetrator blast-fragmentation, ~450 kg (WDU-42/B, shared with JASSM) —
  [Missile Threat / CSIS](https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/agm-158c-lrasm/)
- Platforms: F/A-18E/F Super Hornet, F-35C, B-1B Lancer (surface-ship launch in development) —
  [Missile Threat / CSIS](https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/agm-158c-lrasm/)

## Visible parts / what it looks like in action

- Slender, faceted cruise-missile body derived from JASSM: a flattened, low-observable fuselage cross-section,
  small folded wings and tail fins that deploy after release, and a chin-mounted sensor window.
- Air-launched: drops clean from an aircraft weapons bay or wing pylon, wings snap out, then a short rocket-motor
  ignition kicks the missile onto its cruise flight path — no visible smoke trail once at cruise altitude, unlike
  a boosted vertical-launch round.
- On the F-35C it carries internally (bay doors open briefly for release); on the Super Hornet and B-1B it is
  visible externally on pylons/rotary launchers before launch.
- No public footage shows terminal approach or impact; released imagery is all pre-launch carriage and the
  moment of release.
