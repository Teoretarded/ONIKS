# Tor-M2 (9K332, SA-15 Gauntlet family)

Role: short-range, mobile, all-weather point air-defence system on a tracked chassis. Protects TELs, troop
concentrations and command posts from aircraft, helicopters, cruise missiles and drones — a low tier the current
roster (S-400 far layer, Pantsir near layer) doesn't quite cover for fast-reacting point defence of ground units.

## Specs

- Crew: 3 — [Army Recognition system profile](https://www.armyrecognition.com/)
- Combat weight: ~34 tonnes (tracked chassis) — [Army Recognition system profile](https://www.armyrecognition.com/)
- Max road speed: ~65 km/h — [Army Recognition system profile](https://www.armyrecognition.com/)
- Missiles carried: 8 ready-to-fire 9M338/9M331 interceptors in two 4-round launch modules — [Army Recognition system profile](https://www.armyrecognition.com/)
- Engagement range: ~1-16 km — [Missile Threat / CSIS SA-15 entry](https://missilethreat.csis.org/defsys/sa-15-gauntlet/)
- Engagement altitude: up to ~10 km — [Missile Threat / CSIS SA-15 entry](https://missilethreat.csis.org/defsys/sa-15-gauntlet/)
- Reaction time: reported ~5-10 seconds from detection to launch — [Missile Threat / CSIS SA-15 entry](https://missilethreat.csis.org/defsys/sa-15-gauntlet/)

## Visible parts / what it looks like in action

- Compact tracked armoured vehicle with a low, rounded turret; a flat circular surveillance radar antenna folds
  down over the front glacis when travelling and rotates up when in action.
- Two missile launch modules sit on top of the turret, each holding four canisters, flanking a separate tracking
  radar dish/optical sight cluster on the turret roof.
- Missile launch is a vertical "pop-up": a missile is ejected a short distance upward by a gas charge before its
  rocket motor ignites and it tips over toward the target — a distinct launch signature from a rail- or
  box-launched round.
- On the move it looks like a small, boxy armoured vehicle; in action the radar dish is up and rotating, a visual
  tell that it is tracking.
