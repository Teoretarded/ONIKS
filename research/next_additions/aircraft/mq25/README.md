# Boeing MQ-25 Stingray

Role: US Navy's first operational carrier-based unmanned aircraft — an aerial tanker that extends the strike-
fighter air wing's range, freeing F/A-18E/F from flying the "buddy tanker" role themselves.

## Specs

- Length: 15.5 m (51 ft) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_MQ-25_Stingray)
- Wingspan: 22.9 m (75 ft) extended, folding to 9.5 m (31.3 ft) for carrier deck/elevator stowage — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_MQ-25_Stingray)
- Fuel offload: capable of delivering ~7,250 kg (16,000 lb) of fuel at up to 500 nmi from the carrier — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_MQ-25_Stingray), [WION](https://www.wionews.com/photos/boeing-s-mq-25-stingray-6-key-features-of-the-us-navy-s-first-operational-carrier-based-drone-1766663098907)
- Max speed: ~620 km/h (385 mph); service ceiling 39,000 ft — [WION](https://www.wionews.com/photos/boeing-s-mq-25-stingray-6-key-features-of-the-us-navy-s-first-operational-carrier-based-drone-1766663098907)
- Unmanned — no onboard crew; one Rolls-Royce AE 3007N turbofan — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_MQ-25_Stingray)
- First flight 19 September 2019; first US Navy MQ-25A test flight April 2026 — [Boeing](https://www.boeing.com/features/2026/04/first-us-navy-mq-25a-stingray-completes-test-flight)

## Shape and layout

Front to back: a blended, chined nose with no cockpit (fully unmanned), a wide flying-wing-like fuselage, long
high-aspect-ratio straight wings that fold upward near the root for carrier stowage, a small dorsal engine intake
above the fuselage, twin canted vertical tails, and — the type's defining visible feature — a hose-and-drogue
aerial refuelling pod slung under the fuselage centreline — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_MQ-25_Stingray).

## Moving parts

- Wings fold upward at roughly mid-span for the carrier hangar/elevator envelope.
- Refuelling pod streams a hose-and-drogue that a receiver aircraft plugs into; the pod itself remains fixed
  under the fuselage.
- Conventional tricycle landing gear; no arrestor hook changes visible in photos (it is carrier-recovery capable).

## What it looks like in action

Taxis and launches/recovers on the carrier deck like a crewed jet but with no canopy or pilot visible — deck crew
in cranials work directly up to the nose gear to marshal it. In the tanker role it holds a steady racetrack pattern
astern of the carrier while a receiving strike fighter closes in on the trailing drogue.
