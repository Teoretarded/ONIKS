# Mil Mi-28N / Mi-28NM (Havoc)

Role: Russian tandem-seat attack helicopter, all-weather/night-capable (the "N" suffix) — coastal armour and
landing-craft suppression alongside Ka-52K. The NM is the current-production deep upgrade.

## Specs

- Length: 17.01 m excluding rotors; main rotor diameter 17.2 m; height 3.82 m — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mil_Mi-28)
- Empty weight: 8,590 kg; max takeoff weight 11,500 kg — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mil_Mi-28)
- Max speed: 320 km/h; range 435 km with 5% fuel reserve — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mil_Mi-28)
- Crew: 2 (pilot, weapons systems officer), tandem seating — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mil_Mi-28)
- Two Klimov VK-2500 turboshafts, 1,636 kW each; Mi-28N adopted October 2009 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mil_Mi-28)

## Shape and layout

Front to back: a stepped tandem cockpit (WSO low/front, pilot raised behind) inside an armoured citadel tub, a
chin-mounted 30 mm 2A42 cannon turret under the nose, stub wings carrying four hardpoints (rocket pods, ATGM
racks), a five-blade main rotor on a mast topped by the round Nadir/N025 radar dome, and a four-blade X-shaped
tail rotor at the end of the boom — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mil_Mi-28), [Army Recognition](https://www.armyrecognition.com/military-products/air/helicopters/attack-helicopters/mil-mi-28a-n-havoc).
The Mi-28NM adds a reshaped nose with a new electro-optical turret and enlarged cockpit windows, plus an enhanced
N025M mast-mounted radar — [The National Interest](https://nationalinterest.org/blog/reboot/putins-killers-sky-russia-upgraded-its-mi-28nm-attack-helicopters-185578).

## Moving parts

- Chin cannon turret trains and elevates independently of the airframe.
- Stub-wing hardpoints carry rocket pods/ATGM launch rails; rockets fire in rippled salvos from the pod tubes.
- Landing gear has stroking (energy-absorbing) oleos rated to a 12 m/s vertical impact.
- Main and tail rotors keep turning during ground shutdown checks; the mast-mounted radar dome is fixed, not
  rotating.

## What it looks like in action

A squat, heavily armoured silhouette with a wide chin turret and bulging stub-wing pylons, flying low and fast;
rocket salvos from the wing pods flash in quick succession rather than a single event. The NM variant's bigger
cockpit windows and reshaped nose sensor housing are the clearest way to tell it apart from the base N at a
glance.
