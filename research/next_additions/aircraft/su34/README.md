# Sukhoi Su-34 (Fullback)

Role: Russian twin-engine, two-seat strike/fighter-bomber — the coastal side's stand-off land-attack aircraft,
distinct from the pure air-superiority Su-35S/Su-57.

## Specs

- Length: 23.34 m; wingspan 14.7 m; height 6.09 m — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukhoi_Su-34)
- Empty weight: 22,500 kg; max takeoff weight 45,100 kg — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukhoi_Su-34)
- Max speed: 1,900 km/h (Mach 1.8); ferry range 4,500 km; combat radius ~1,100 km with a standard load — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukhoi_Su-34)
- Crew: 2, side-by-side seating — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukhoi_Su-34)
- Two Saturn AL-31FM1 afterburning turbofans; introduced 20 March 2014 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukhoi_Su-34)

## Shape and layout

Front to back: a distinctive flattened, duck-billed nose radome housing the phased-array radar, a wide
side-by-side armoured cockpit (17 mm titanium tub) with a flat windscreen, small canard foreplanes ahead of the
wing root, swept wings on 12 hardpoints, twin widely-spaced engines feeding from underslung intakes, and twin
canted tails — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukhoi_Su-34).

## Moving parts

- Canard foreplanes are fixed aerodynamic surfaces (not variable-sweep), adding lift/stability at low altitude.
- Twin-wheel nose gear and reinforced tandem-wheel main gear, sized for the aircraft's high max takeoff weight,
  retract into the fuselage/wing roots.
- 12 external hardpoints carry ordnance — no internal weapons bay.
- Boom-refuelling probe extends from the nose for aerial refuelling.

## What it looks like in action

The flattened nose and side-by-side canopy give it a blunter, wider-fronted silhouette than any single-seat
Flanker variant — easy to tell apart from Su-30SM2/Su-35S at a glance. On the ramp the crew cabin is tall enough
that groundcrew visibly work at head height around the canopy rather than climbing a ladder to a narrow cockpit.
