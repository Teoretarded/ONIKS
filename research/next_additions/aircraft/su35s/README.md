# Sukhoi Su-35S (Flanker-E)

Role: Russia's top 4++-generation single-seat, twin-engine multirole fighter — air superiority and strike, with
thrust-vectoring engines for extreme close-in agility. Sits below the Su-57 as the coastal side's numerous,
affordable air-superiority workhorse, escorting Su-34/Su-30SM2 strike packages.

## Specs

- Length: 21.9 m — [Airforce Technology](https://www.airforce-technology.com/projects/su-35/)
- Wingspan: 15.3 m — [Airforce Technology](https://www.airforce-technology.com/projects/su-35/)
- Max takeoff weight: ~34,500 kg — [Airforce Technology](https://www.airforce-technology.com/projects/su-35/)
- Max speed: ~2,400 km/h (Mach ~2.25) — [Airforce Technology](https://www.airforce-technology.com/projects/su-35/)
- Range: ~4,500 km — [Airforce Technology](https://www.airforce-technology.com/projects/su-35/)
- Service entry: 2014, Russian Aerospace Forces — [Airforce Technology](https://www.airforce-technology.com/projects/su-35/)

## Visible parts / what it looks like in action

- Classic Flanker silhouette scaled up: twin canted tails, long wing-root strakes (LERX), and two widely-spaced
  AL-41F1S engines with round, thrust-vectoring exhaust nozzles — a rounder, non-stealth profile next to the
  Su-57's faceted shaping.
- All-external weapon carriage: 12 hardpoints under wings/fuselage, no internal bays — missiles hang visibly on
  pylons rather than behind bay doors.
- In display flight it performs the cobra (nose past vertical, near-zero forward speed) and the kulbit (a tight
  vertical loop with minimal radius) using nozzle vectoring — a distinctive low-speed, high-alpha flight signature.
- On the ramp: twin ventral fins under the tail boom and a long, pointed nose radome are the easiest silhouette
  cues versus the Su-57/Su-30SM2 already in the roster.
