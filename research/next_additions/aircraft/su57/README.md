# Sukhoi Su-57 (Felon)

Role: Russia's only operational 5th-generation stealth fighter, single-seat, twin-engine, multirole air
superiority/strike. The coastal-defence side's natural counter to the carrier group's F-35C.

## Specs

- Length: 20.1 m — [Sukhoi/UAC public specifications, cited via Flightglobal/Jane's](https://www.rostec.ru/en/)
- Wingspan: 14.1 m — [Rostec/UAC specifications](https://www.rostec.ru/en/)
- Max takeoff weight: ~35,000 kg — [Rostec/UAC specifications](https://www.rostec.ru/en/)
- Max speed: ~Mach 2 (~2,135 km/h) at altitude — [Rostec/UAC specifications](https://www.rostec.ru/en/)
- Combat radius: ~1,200 km (subsonic) — [Rostec/UAC specifications](https://www.rostec.ru/en/)
- Service entry: December 2020, Russian Aerospace Forces — [TASS](https://tass.com/defense/1235957)
- Internal + external weapon stations: two ventral internal bays plus wing-root bays, 6 external hardpoints when
  stealth is not required — [Naval News / Air Force Technology overview](https://www.airforce-technology.com/projects/pakfa/)

## Visible parts / what it looks like in action

- Faceted, all-aspect stealth-shaped fuselage with sharply blended wing-body chines, twin canted vertical tails,
  and two widely-spaced engine nacelles with trapezoidal exhaust nozzles.
- Internal weapon bays (ventral, between the engines, plus small wing-root bays) open briefly for a weapon
  release — a fast, flush-panel action rather than the AIM-120's simple pylon drop.
- On the ground/at airshows it displays a low, wide stance and its two large split main gear doors; distinctive
  serrated trailing edges on flight-control surfaces are a visible stealth-shaping cue.
- In flight it shows notable post-stall agility (thrust-vectoring nozzles) at airshow displays, a different flight
  signature from the Su-35S already in the roster.
