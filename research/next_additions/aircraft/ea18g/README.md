# Boeing EA-18G Growler

Role: carrier-based electronic-attack derivative of the F/A-18F Super Hornet, replacing the EA-6B Prowler. Flies
ahead of and alongside a strike package to jam enemy radars/comms and fire anti-radiation missiles — the visual
"escort jamming" role the current roster (F/A-18E, F-35C) doesn't cover.

## Specs

- Length: 18.5 m (60.2 ft) — [Airforce Technology](https://www.airforce-technology.com/projects/ea-18g-growler-electronic-attack-aircraft/)
- Wingspan: 13.68 m (44.9 ft) — [Airforce Technology](https://www.airforce-technology.com/projects/ea-18g-growler-electronic-attack-aircraft/)
- Max speed: Mach 1.8 — [Airforce Technology](https://www.airforce-technology.com/projects/ea-18g-growler-electronic-attack-aircraft/)
- Combat range: 850+ nautical miles with a jamming/HARM loadout — [Airforce Technology](https://www.airforce-technology.com/projects/ea-18g-growler-electronic-attack-aircraft/)
- Powerplant: 2x F414-GE-400 turbofans, 22,000 lb thrust each — [Airforce Technology](https://www.airforce-technology.com/projects/ea-18g-growler-electronic-attack-aircraft/)
- Armament: AGM-88 HARM, AIM-120, plus up to 3 ALQ-99 or AN/ALQ-249 (NGJ-MB) jamming pods — [NAVAIR](https://www.navair.navy.mil/product/EA-18G-Growler)

## Visible parts / what it looks like in action

- Near-identical two-seat Super Hornet airframe, distinguished by two large finned jamming pods on the outboard
  wing pylons (older ALQ-99, cylindrical with a small nose propeller-generator; newer NGJ-MB, blockier fairing) and
  wingtip pods replacing the AIM-9 rail.
- Carrier ops are the same catapult/wire cycle already modelled for F/A-18E/F-35C, but the jamming pods are the
  clear "this one's a Growler" read at a distance.
- In an escort role it typically flies loose formation ahead of/beside the strike group rather than in the strike
  formation itself — a positioning cue as much as a visual one.
- No visible effect from active jamming itself (it's RF, not optical); the pods and formation position are the
  only in-game-readable tells.
