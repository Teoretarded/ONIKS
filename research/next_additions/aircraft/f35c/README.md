# F-35C Lightning II

Role: carrier-based variant of the F-35 family, single-seat stealth strike fighter built for catapult launch and
arrested landing. Completes the carrier air wing alongside the F/A-18E and EA-18G already in the roster, adding a
stealthy first-day-of-strike capability distinct from the Super Hornet.

## Specs

- Length: 15.7 m — [Lockheed Martin F-35C fact sheet](https://www.f35.com/f35/about/variants/f-35c.html)
- Wingspan: 13.1 m (folded for carrier stowage) — [Lockheed Martin F-35C fact sheet](https://www.f35.com/f35/about/variants/f-35c.html)
- Max takeoff weight: ~31,800 kg — [Lockheed Martin F-35C fact sheet](https://www.f35.com/f35/about/variants/f-35c.html)
- Max speed: ~Mach 1.6 — [Lockheed Martin F-35C fact sheet](https://www.f35.com/f35/about/variants/f-35c.html)
- Combat radius: ~670+ nautical miles (~1,240 km) internal fuel — [Lockheed Martin F-35C fact sheet](https://www.f35.com/f35/about/variants/f-35c.html)
- First carrier trap/launch: USS *Nimitz*, November 2014 — [US Navy release](https://www.navair.navy.mil/)
- Internal weapon stations: two internal bays (up to 4 air-to-air or a mixed air-to-ground load); 6 external
  wing stations when stealth is not required — [Lockheed Martin F-35C fact sheet](https://www.f35.com/f35/about/variants/f-35c.html)

## Visible parts / what it looks like in action

- Larger wings and reinforced landing gear versus the F-35A/B, with a tailhook under the aft fuselage for
  arrested landings — the visible giveaway that separates it from the land-based F-35A.
- Single engine, faceted stealth fuselage, diverterless supersonic inlet (a bump rather than a moving ramp) on
  each side of the nose.
- Catapult launch: nose-gear tow bar hooks the catapult shuttle, a steam/electromagnetic launch stroke flings it
  off the bow in under 2 seconds, canopy and control surfaces visibly load up under the acceleration.
- Recovery: tailhook catches an arresting wire, an abrupt deceleration with the aircraft's nose dipping as it
  snaps to a stop — a sharply different silhouette and motion beat from a runway landing.
