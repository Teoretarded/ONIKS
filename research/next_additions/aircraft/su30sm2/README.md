# Sukhoi Su-30SM2 (Flanker-H upgrade)

Role: deep upgrade of Russia's two-seat Su-30SM multirole fighter, re-engined and re-radared with Su-35S-family
components — a "Super Sukhoi" bridge between the SM and the single-seat Su-35S/Su-57 already covered.

## Specs

- Length: 21.935 m; wingspan 14.7 m; height 6.36 m — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukhoi_Su-30SM)
- Empty weight: ~17,700 kg; max takeoff weight ~34,500 kg — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukhoi_Su-30SM)
- Max speed: 2,120 km/h (Mach ~2) at altitude; range ~3,000 km at high altitude (base Su-30SM figures; the
  sources checked don't give SM2-specific speed/range) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukhoi_Su-30SM)
- Crew: 2 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukhoi_Su-30SM)
- Two AL-41F-1S afterburning turbofans with thrust vectoring (upgraded from the SM's AL-31FL/FP); Irbis-E radar
  from the Su-35S; first deliveries 2021-2022 — [Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/russia-produces-a-new-batch-of-su-30sm2-multi-role-fighters/), [Army Recognition](https://www.armyrecognition.com/news/aerospace-news/2024/upgraded-su-30sm2-fighters-join-russian-aerospace-forces)

## Shape and layout

Front to back: shares the Su-30SM's twin-seat, twin-tail Flanker airframe — a pointed nose radome, tandem
cockpit under a long single-piece canopy, wing-root leading-edge extensions (LERX), swept wings with underwing
and wingtip hardpoints, twin widely-spaced engines, and twin canted tails with a tail boom between them — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukhoi_Su-30SM).
The SM2's visible difference from the base SM is round, thrust-vectoring engine nozzles (shared with the Su-35S)
in place of the SM's fixed nozzles — [Key.Aero](https://www.key.aero/article/depth-look-russias-enhanced-sukhoi-su-30sm2).

## Moving parts

- Thrust-vectoring nozzles pivot in flight, giving the extreme high-alpha manoeuvres (cobra, kulbit) also flown
  by the Su-35S.
- Wingtip and underwing pylons carry air-to-air ordnance; the aircraft has no internal weapons bay.
- Canopy hinges open rearward for the tandem crew.

## What it looks like in action

Visually near-identical to the base Su-30SM at a distance — same twin-tail, twin-seat Flanker silhouette already
in the roster via the Su-35S family — so the tell is the round vectoring nozzles up close, plus the newer
Irbis-E-driven engagement behaviour rather than anything externally dramatic.
