# Admiral Grigorovich class (Project 11356)

Role: Russia's Black Sea Fleet workhorse frigate, a Krivak-derived hull that predates the Gorshkov class already
in the roster. Smaller and simpler than Gorshkov, giving the coastal side a second, more numerous escort tier —
6 planned, 3 in Russian service after 3 hulls were redirected to India as the *Talwar*-class follow-on.

## Specs

- Length: 124.8 m — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Admiral_Grigorovich-class_frigate)
- Beam: 15.2 m — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Admiral_Grigorovich-class_frigate)
- Displacement: 4,000 tonnes full load — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Admiral_Grigorovich-class_frigate)
- Complement: ~200 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Admiral_Grigorovich-class_frigate)
- Armament: 8-cell UKSK VLS (Kalibr/Oniks/Zircon), 24-cell Shtil-1 SAM, 1x 100 mm A-190E gun, 2x AK-630 CIWS, twin
  torpedo tubes, RBU-6000, 1x Ka-27 helicopter — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Admiral_Grigorovich-class_frigate)
- Lead ship commissioned: 2016 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Admiral_Grigorovich-class_frigate)

## Visible parts / what it looks like in action

- Krivak-family hull with a lower, more traditional radar-and-mast superstructure than Gorshkov's flat-panel
  Poliment array — a rounder, less angular silhouette next to the newer class.
- Forward 8-cell UKSK hatch block just ahead of the bridge (half the cell count of Gorshkov's 16), a separate
  Shtil-1 SAM deck section further aft, and a single 100 mm gun on the bow.
- Twin funnel/mast arrangement amidships (COGAG propulsion venting) is a visible difference from Gorshkov's
  single enclosed mast.
- Underway it shows the same grey Black Sea Fleet paint scheme; the visible cue against Gorshkov is the shorter
  hull, lower profile and twin-funnel silhouette.
