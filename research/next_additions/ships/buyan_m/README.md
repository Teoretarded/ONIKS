# Buyan-M class (Project 21631)

Role: small, shallow-draft "river-sea" missile corvette that punches far above its size — the platform that made
Kalibr famous, firing from the Caspian and Black Seas. Gives the coastal side a cheap, numerous, hard-to-target
missile carrier distinct from the larger Gorshkov/Grigorovich frigates.

## Specs

- Length: 74.1 m — [Naval News](https://www.navalnews.com/naval-news/2024/06/russia-launches-final-buyan-m-class-corvette-fitted-with-kalibr-cruise-missiles/)
- Beam: 11 m — [Naval News](https://www.navalnews.com/naval-news/2024/06/russia-launches-final-buyan-m-class-corvette-fitted-with-kalibr-cruise-missiles/)
- Displacement: 949 tonnes full load — [USNI Proceedings, Dec 2024](https://www.usni.org/magazines/proceedings/2024/december/russias-buyan-naval-corvette-program)
- Speed: 25 knots max, 2,500 nm range at 12 knots — [USNI Proceedings](https://www.usni.org/magazines/proceedings/2024/december/russias-buyan-naval-corvette-program)
- Armament: 8-cell 3S14 UKSK VLS (Kalibr/Oniks/Zircon-capable), 1x 100 mm A-190 gun — [USNI Proceedings](https://www.usni.org/magazines/proceedings/2024/december/russias-buyan-naval-corvette-program)
- Final hull (*Stavropol*) commissioned: 2025 — [Army Recognition](https://www.armyrecognition.com/news/navy-news/2025/russia-commissions-final-project-21631-buyan-m-corvette-stavropol-to-support-baltic-operations)

## Visible parts / what it looks like in action

- Tiny for its firepower: a low, flat-decked hull barely longer than the corvette-class Karakurt, riverine-derived
  and shallow-draft — reads as a small patrol boat until the VLS hatches are spotted.
- Flush 8-cell UKSK hatch block forward of a compact bridge, a single 100 mm gun on the bow, and a boxy, low
  superstructure with minimal mast — no helicopter facilities at all.
- Kalibr launch: identical cold-launch/gas-ejection signature to the larger ships' 3S14 cells, but visually
  dramatic on such a small hull — the missile dwarfing the deck it leaves from.
- Underway in coastal/river-mouth waters it sits very low and flat on the water, a visibly different wake and
  freeboard from any frigate-sized hull in the roster.
