# Karakurt class (Project 22800)

Role: small, stealth-shaped missile corvette designed as the open-water-capable successor to Buyan-M — same
Kalibr punch, sturdier hull for the Baltic/Black Sea/Pacific rather than river-sea routes. Some hulls (*Odintsovo*)
carry the Pantsir-M naval CIWS, a point-defence layer not yet modelled on any small combatant in the roster.

## Specs

- Length: ~67 m — [Naval Technology](https://www.naval-technology.com/projects/project-22800-karakurt-class-corvettes/)
- Beam: 11 m — [Naval Technology](https://www.naval-technology.com/projects/project-22800-karakurt-class-corvettes/)
- Displacement: 860 tonnes full load — [Naval Technology](https://www.naval-technology.com/projects/project-22800-karakurt-class-corvettes/)
- Speed: up to 35 knots — [Naval Technology](https://www.naval-technology.com/projects/project-22800-karakurt-class-corvettes/)
- Armament: 8-cell UKSK VLS (Kalibr-NK), 1x 76.2 mm AK-176MA or 100 mm A-190 gun, some hulls with Pantsir-M CIWS
  instead of a second gun — [Naval Technology](https://www.naval-technology.com/projects/project-22800-karakurt-class-corvettes/)
- *Odintsovo* (first Pantsir-M-armed hull) commissioned: November 2020 — [Naval News](https://www.navalnews.com/naval-news/2020/11/karakurt-class-corvette-odintsovo-commissioned-with-russias-baltic-fleet-part-2/)

## Visible parts / what it looks like in action

- Almaz-designed stealth-shaped hull: sloped, faceted topsides and a single integrated mast carrying phased-array
  radar panels — a sharper, more angular small-ship silhouette than Buyan-M's boxy hull.
- Forward 8-cell UKSK hatch block, a single gun forward of the bridge, and — on Pantsir-M hulls — a distinctive
  turret aft with twin gun barrels and vertical missile cells in place of a second deck gun.
- Pantsir-M engagement: the turret slews and its twin autocannon/missile combination tracks a close-in air target,
  a visually different point-defence beat from the Tor-M2/Buk-family systems already covered.
- Underway it sits low and lean like Buyan-M but with a more angular, harder-edged hull line — reads as the
  "stealthy" small corvette next to the riverine Buyan-M.
