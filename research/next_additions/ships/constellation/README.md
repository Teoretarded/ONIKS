# Constellation class (FFG-62)

Role: new-build US Navy guided-missile frigate, based on the Italian/French FREMM design, meant to fill the
escort gap left as older frigates retired. Lead ship *Constellation* laid down 2022; the class fills a real
capability slot around a US carrier group that the LCS in the current roster does not fully cover.

## Specs

- Length: ~496 ft (~151 m) — [US Navy fact file, NAVSEA](https://www.navsea.navy.mil/Media/Press-Room/Fact-Files/Display-FactFiles/Article/2169237/guided-missile-frigate-ffg-62-class/)
- Beam: ~65 ft (~19.8 m) — [US Navy fact file, NAVSEA](https://www.navsea.navy.mil/Media/Press-Room/Fact-Files/Display-FactFiles/Article/2169237/guided-missile-frigate-ffg-62-class/)
- Displacement: ~7,300 tons full load — [US Navy fact file, NAVSEA](https://www.navsea.navy.mil/Media/Press-Room/Fact-Files/Display-FactFiles/Article/2169237/guided-missile-frigate-ffg-62-class/)
- Speed: 26+ knots — [US Navy fact file, NAVSEA](https://www.navsea.navy.mil/Media/Press-Room/Fact-Files/Display-FactFiles/Article/2169237/guided-missile-frigate-ffg-62-class/)
- Complement: ~200 — [US Navy fact file, NAVSEA](https://www.navsea.navy.mil/Media/Press-Room/Fact-Files/Display-FactFiles/Article/2169237/guided-missile-frigate-ffg-62-class/)
- Armament: 32-cell Mk 41 VLS (ESSM/SM-2/SM-6/Tomahawk-capable), 16 NSM canisters, 57 mm Mk 110 gun, SeaRAM,
  MH-60R/MQ-8 aviation facilities — [US Navy fact file, NAVSEA](https://www.navsea.navy.mil/Media/Press-Room/Fact-Files/Display-FactFiles/Article/2169237/guided-missile-frigate-ffg-62-class/)

## Visible parts / what it looks like in action

- FREMM-derived hull: a distinctive stepped, angular superstructure with a single lattice-free enclosed mast,
  visually different from the boxier Burke/Ticonderoga already in the roster.
- Forward 32-cell Mk 41 VLS block just ahead of the bridge, a bank of angled NSM canister launchers amidships
  (same launch-box language as the LCS NSM fit), and a 57 mm gun on the bow.
- Flight deck and enclosed hangar aft sized for one MH-60R plus an MQ-8 unmanned helicopter, distinguishing its
  silhouette from the single-spot LCS flight deck.
- As of 2026 no hull has been commissioned; all public imagery is artist renderings and shipyard model views
  rather than at-sea photography.
