1. **High · R on a TEL said "TLV TO TEL 04" while the transloader was already on its way to TEL 03**; the TEL then
   drove 18 km to the depot instead (sim sends a TEL to a depot when no transloader is idle). Repro: empty both TELs, R
   on TEL 03, then R on TEL 04. `game/orders.js reload()`. **Fixed** (see below).
2. **High · SM-6 hit rate is low, and slightly worse at close crossings since 0c39260.** Measured headless (same
   geometry as the test, Oniks at 15 m, one SM-6, track error on, 5 seeds × 12 geometries): kills 2/60 now vs 2/60 before;
   typical pass 20-50 m (burst radius 8 m, sig0 14 m). Close crossings (4-8 km abeam, 8-16 km out): gross misses > 300 m
   in 6/12 cases now vs 5/12 before (e.g. 4 km abeam / 12 km out: 1.8 km now, 1.0 km before). The eased pitch-over
   costs the close-in shot a little; it does not change the overall rate. In play 4 Oniks took 8 SM-6 (2 direct, 2 burst
   spin-outs, 3 clean misses, 1 self-destruct). `sim/weapons.js`, PROJ.sm6 (balance: not retuned).
3. **Med · SPECTATE banner sat on the edge-pinned enemy track tags** (top centre, "TRK 23 · DDG ARLEIGH BURKE"). Repro:
   double-click any own unit with enemy ships off screen. `game/select.js`. **Fixed.**
4. **Med · `[` / `]` ignored when the key arrives without a code** (remote tools, some IMEs): the rate stayed x4.
   `game/time.js`. **Fixed** (falls back to the key character).

   ~2 min per round. At x4 that is 4 real minutes of waiting with no ETA shown. `sim/orders.js parkBy`.
6. **Med · Salvo mode: clicking a track on the scope / minimap moves the camera instead of firing.** At 50 km the Orbital
   glyph works; the scope does not. `ui/hud/minimap.js`, `game/orders.js targeting`.
7. **Low · Orbital layer shows each ship twice**: the catalog label at the glyph ("DDG · Arleigh Burke · stopped") and the
