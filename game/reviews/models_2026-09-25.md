- ssn774 README: "X-stern": Virginia Blocks I-IV have a cruciform stern (the model is right).
- kilo636 README: "sail-mounted planes on 636.3": the planes are on the bow casing.
- bastion / bal README: 11.2 m length: the MZKT-7930 alone is ~12.7 m; with the pack the TEL is ~13.8 m (as modelled).
