## 1. Hypervelocity impact / plate perforation
Sources
- NASA Ames Vertical Gun Range factsheet (to ~7 km/s, 15-degree steps, Phantom + Shimadzu HPV1 cameras): https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/range_factsheet_i.pdf
- AVGR imaging upgrade (50 um particles at 2 km/s visible): https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20180007384/downloads/20180007384.pdf
- AGU abstract, radiometer impact flash 350-1100 nm, Pyrex into pumice 1-6 km/s: https://agu.confex.com/agu/fm15/webprogram/Paper69894.html (not fetchable; seen in search only)
- Whipple shield >9 km/s regimes (ballistic / shatter / melt-vapour), NASA JSC: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20090024821/downloads/20090024821.pdf (figures not read)
- Laser shadowgraphs of debris clouds, 10-15 km/s: https://iafastro.directory/iac/archive/browse/IAC-19/A6/3/52493/
- Active Whipple shield shadowgraph figures: https://www.bzycj.cn/cn/article/doi/10.11883/bzycj-2020-0266

Look sheet
- Flash: 6.4 mm Al into Al at 6.3 km/s: strong flash ~10 us, ~4300 K [src, search snippet]. Iron-projectile modelling: ~1 us [src]. Lower ambient pressure lengthens the flash [src].
- Onset: a luminous disc expands around the impactor, a few us later it turns "floral" (petal/jet fragmentation) [src, 3 km/s steel into Al].
- Colour: near-white core (#FFFFFF-#FFF3D6) cooling to yellow-orange (#FFC24A -> #FF7A1F) in microseconds; alloy lines can tint (Zn in Al-7075 gave orange-yellow) [src]. Real flash is far shorter than a film beat, so stretch it: 2-4 frames white, then 10-20 frames orange fade at 60 fps [est].
- Shapes [est, standard Whipple shadowgraph look]: bubble-shaped debris cloud behind the plate with a denser leading cap of projectile fragments and a thin expanding shell of plate material; ejecta cone/splash back toward the shooter; hole slightly larger than the rod, rim petals bent outward on the exit side, bright molten rim for a moment.
- Spray: incandescent particles streak in straight lines (no visible gravity at this scale), fade orange -> dull red -> dark in ~0.3-1 s [est].

## 2. Large ships exploding
Sources
- USS Arizona forward-magazine blast, colour film from hospital ship Solace; stills NH 80-G-K-13512/13513; the film is often shown mirrored: https://ibiblio.org/hyperwar/OnlineLibrary/photos/images/k13000/k13513c.htm ; https://www.usni.org/magazines/naval-history-magazine/2001/december/our-scope
- USS Mount Hood (AE-11) 10 Nov 1944, Manus, ~4,500 t explosives, 0803; stills NH 96173 (smoke trails of thrown fragments), 96174, 96175, 95404-95406, 65605: https://ibiblio.org/hyperwar/OnlineLibrary/photos/images/h96000/h96173c.htm ; https://www.usni.org/magazines/proceedings/1963/february/mount-hood-explosion (403 to my fetch)
- HMS Barham 25 Nov 1941, Pathe/John Turner film; stock log puts the blast at 04:10:48 inside a 04:10:04-04:11:28 clip: https://footagefarm.com/reel-details/wwii/generic/wwii---1941-england--spectacular-films--sinking-british-warship-explodes ; https://www.royalmarineshistory.com/post/hms-barham-126-royal-marines-lost
- SINKEX: ex-Durham, 3 missiles amidships in a tight cluster, 4th near miss at ~12-14 s of clip: https://www.twz.com/36077/watch-three-anti-ship-missiles-rip-through-uss-durham-during-rimpac-sinking-exercise ; ex-Ingraham broken in two by a Mk48: https://www.twz.com/42129/watch-the-ex-uss-ingraham-frigate-get-its-back-broken-by-a-torpedo ; ex-Boone fireball: https://taskandpurpose.com/news/atlantic-thunder-sinkex

Look sheet
- Barham: torpedoes ~16:25, ship capsized, magazine blast ~4 min later, sank within ~4 min after [src, sources disagree].
- Arizona/Barham/Mount Hood pattern [est from memory of the footage, verify on playback]: (1) instantaneous white-yellow flash/shell front; (2) dome/cauliflower fireball yellow-orange (#FFD060 -> #FF8A1E) swelling ~1-2 s; (3) dense black-brown column (#1A1512) rising and billowing with the base rolling inward, ~20-40 m/s early, slowing; (4) debris on parabolic arcs with thin smoke trails (Mount Hood NH 96173 shows exactly these trails) [src]; (5) the pillar keeps rising 10-30 s, sometimes with a white condensation cap/skirt in humid air.
- Chain of blasts: secondaries are separate cauliflower balls 0.2-1 s apart, each smaller and more orange, flame licking along the hull from vents; the hull sags and splits at the blast, bow and stern settle apart over tens of seconds [est].
- SINKEX missile hits: short orange flash out of the hull side with a black smoke jet; fires stay red-orange inside the hull, then dark smoke for minutes [est].

## 3. Staged practical explosions
Sources
- Spectre base blast: 8,418 L kerosene, 24 individually timed charges, ~7.5 s duration, ~68 t TNT-equivalent claim: https://nofilmschool.com/2015/11/new-james-bond-film-set-world-record-biggest-movie-explosion-ever
- Inception street: air-cannon lightweight debris, shot at 700 fps; Zabriskie Point house blast as reference: https://digital.copcomm.com/i/17873/13 ; https://www.timeout.com/film/the-hair-raising-stories-behind-the-biggest-explosions-in-movies
- Dark Knight hospital: two phases (cosmetic directional effect, then real demolition): https://cultbox.co.uk/interviews/syndicated/spectre-interview-special-effects-supervisor-chris-corbould

Look sheet [est unless stated]
- Layers: kerosene/gas fireball (slow, rolling, orange, black soot tips) + lightweight debris (cork, fibre, dust) + bright spark streaks + dust/smoke body + a shockwave seen only as a dust/water ring and a push on vegetation.
- Timing: charges sequenced 50-300 ms apart so the fireball elongates along the target; at 700-1000 fps shown at 24 fps the first 0.5 s fills ~10-20 s of screen; the fireball shell peels outward, debris hangs, then drifts.
- Fireball colours: core #FFF2C0, body #FFB030, edges #E8501A, soot #1B1512.

Look sheet
- Baker: bubble reaches the surface at ms scale; spray dome (~2 Mt water claimed) rising initially ~2500 ft/s (~760 m/s) [unverified secondary]; hollow column ~1.8 km tall by ~0.6 km wide [src, sources conflict]; then a base surge. Far above rod scale, so borrow only the SHAPE: smooth dome, then hollow column with bright white-grey wall, falling spray ring.
- Fast rod into sea [est]: ring crater and crown of white spray over 0.1-0.5 s, hollow cavity glimpsed, then jet and white water column; steam bright white-grey, spray white with a blue-green tinge; a hot rod boils a lumpy white bubble cloud for seconds.

## 5. Re-entry / meteor glow
Sources
- Hayabusa capsule return 13 Jun 2010, ~12.2 km/s, luminous ~60-70 s, NASA DC-8 imaging and spectra of capsule, wake and bus breakup: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20110013225 ; https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20110015027 (PDF text unreadable in my fetch)
- Fireball colours (Na yellow-orange, Mg green/blue-white, Fe, Ni green): https://impact.uwo.ca/fireballs ; https://apod.nasa.gov/rjn/apod/ap211220.html

Look sheet [est]
- Head: small blinding blue-white core (#E8F1FF), teardrop sheath 2-3 body widths, orange-yellow (#FFB347) wake; ablation sheds a train of sparks that fan out and cool yellow -> red; faint green/teal persistent train for seconds.
- A dense rod at 10+ km/s lights from ~100 km down; below ~30 km the sheath turns violet-white with a visible compressed air cone.
