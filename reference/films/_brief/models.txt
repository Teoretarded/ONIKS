HD MODEL KIT for the extended menu cutscenes
============================================

Two files, two builders, one shared namespace:
  common/hd_land.js     -> builder "land":    HD.tel, HD.telTube, HD.oniks, HD.oniksBooster, HD.radar, HD.pantsir, HD.sm6, HD.mk72 + helpers
  common/hd_sea_air.js  -> builder "sea/air": HD.destroyer, HD.helo, HD.fighter, HD.drone, HD.catapult, HD.satellite, HD.carrier
Each file starts with `window.HD = window.HD || {};` and ends by setting HD.READY_LAND = true / HD.READY_SEA_AIR = true.
Both load after ../menus/common/m3.js and ../menus/common/geo.js and must not depend on each other at load time
(hd_sea_air.js may use HD.wheel etc. only if present, with a local fallback).
Write the file in one go when a first complete, working version exists (films are waiting to switch over),
then keep improving it in place. Never break the API below once published.

Primitives (GEO, menus/common/geo.js): hex, box, lathe, cyl, panel, line (polyline; w = alpha scale),
blades (fans/props/rotors: c, d, n, r0, r1, {chord, taper, pitch, edge, rib, hub, shroud, rot}), hull (GEO's ship hull).
Part = { name, label, prims | dyn(st) -> prims, xf?(st) -> rigid X, show?(st) -> bool, alpha? }.
  dyn(st) rebuilds prims from state (telescoping rams, folding wings, spinning blades: put rot into blades()).
Prim options: fine: true = small detail skipped by GEO.draw(..., {fine:false}) / GEO.sample(m, s, seed, st, {fine:false});
  pts: false = not sampled into dots; ds = sampling density multiplier; al = alpha; gen/rings/n for lathes.
Renderers: Orbital films draw GEO.draw(W, model, worldX, st, opt) as facing-aware hairlines;
  Point Cloud films GEO.sample(model, spacing, seed, st) -> per-part points (+normals) and project them as dots.
  A model must look right in BOTH: clean readable hairlines (no moire from too many parallel lines; use gen/rings
  sparingly, hatch for panels) and a good dot surface (closed shapes, sensible normals, panels only where thin).

Conventions: metres, +Z forward, +Y up, X to the right-hand side... (X east when unrotated, see m3.js).
Vehicles: origin on the ground at the vehicle centre. Missiles/aircraft: origin at the middle of the body, nose +Z.
Real dimensions. Every part has a `label` with its real designation (films use them for identification boxes).
Keep the part names below stable: films key off them.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LAND (common/hd_land.js)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HD.tel()  K340P TEL (Bastion-P) on MZKT-7930 8x8. Drop-in for GEO.tel():
  same PIV [0,1.55,-6.7], TUBE [.66,2.5,-6.6], TUBE_LEN 9.25, state elev (rad; 0 stowed .. ~1.53 vertical), dep (0..1 jacks down).
  extra state: capL, capR (0 fitted .. 1 gone: front end caps of the two TLCs), wheel (rad, rolling), fan (rad).
  parts: chassis, wheels, cab, launcher (TLC pair; rotates about PIV), capL, capR (launcher frame), ram (hydraulic erector,
         telescoping, attached at both ends at every elev), jacks (outriggers + pads, move with dep).
  detail: tyres with tread, rims/hubs/lug rings, fenders, fuel tanks, battery/tool boxes, exhaust stack, split windscreen,
          side windows, mirrors on arms, grille, bumper, lights, beacon, whip antenna (line), ladder, TLC lifting lugs,
          cable trays, cap bolt rings, rear TLC closures.
HD.telTube(m, st, side) -> {base, mouth, dir} in model space (same as GEO.tubeFrame; side 1 = right tube, -1 = left).

HD.oniks(o?)  P-800 Oniks / 3M55. Origin mid-body, nose +Z, length 8.9 m, body Ø 0.7 m (match GEO.oniks extents).
  state: wing (0 folded .. 1 deployed), fin (0 folded .. 1), booster (bool: solid booster still in the ramjet chamber,
         its nozzle protrudes from the ramjet nozzle), cover (bool: intake cover fitted, jettisoned at launch).
  parts: body (joint rings, antenna fairings, cable duct), intake (annular nose inlet: lip + conical centre body =
         seeker radome), wings (two mid-body folding wings), fins (4 cruciform tail fins, folding), nozzle (ramjet bell),
         booster (inner booster nozzle), cover.
  constants: HD.oniks.NOZZLE (exit centre), HD.oniks.NOZZLE_R, HD.oniks.INTAKE (lip centre), HD.oniks.LEN.
HD.oniksBooster()  the ejected booster casing with its nozzle; origin at its middle, +Z = its front.

HD.radar()  Monolith-B-class coastal search radar on an 8x8. Drop-in for GEO.radarTruck(): state ant (rad).
  extra state: mast (0 stowed .. 1 raised; default 1).
  parts: chassis, wheels, cab, body (shelter: doors, vents, A/C units, cable reels), mast (telescoping), array (planar
         array: dipole rows, feed, back truss, IFF strip, rotary joint; turns with ant).
  constant: HD.radar.ARRAY (array centre, raised, ant = 0).

HD.pantsir()  Pantsir-S1 on KamAZ-6560 8x8. state: yaw (turret), pitch (guns + missile packs), sAnt (search radar),
  fire (0..1 recoil pulse). parts: chassis, wheels, cab, turret, guns (2x 2A38M twin 30 mm), missiles (2 packs x 6 57E6
  TLCs), searchRadar (turns with sAnt), trackRadar (front phased array), eo.
  functions: HD.pantsir.muzzles(st) -> 4 points; HD.pantsir.tubes(st) -> 12 {mouth, dir}.

HD.sm6(o?)  RIM-174 SM-6 with Mk 72 booster. Origin mid (whole stack), nose +Z. state: booster (bool attached).
  parts: radome, body (with the long low dorsal strakes), fins, mk72 (booster Ø 0.53 m, 4 fins, 4-nozzle cluster), nozzle.
  constants: HD.sm6.NOZZLES (4 booster nozzle exits), HD.sm6.SUSTAINER (sustainer nozzle exit), HD.sm6.LEN.
HD.mk72()  ejected booster, origin its middle.

Helpers (exported for everyone): HD.wheel(prims, z, side, o), HD.chassis8x8(prims, zFront, zRear, o),
HD.ram(B, A, radii, lmin) -> prims, HD.tp(X, prim) -> transformed prim copy.

SEA / AIR (common/hd_sea_air.js)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HD.destroyer()  Arleigh Burke Flight IIA, 155 m x 20 m, origin midships on the waterline, bow +Z.
  Drop-in part names from GEO.destroyer (hull, super, mast, arms) plus:
  spy (4 octagonal SPY-1D faces), stacks (2 funnels with uptake grilles), sps (surface-search radar on the mast, turns with
  state sps), gun (Mk 45 5"/62; state gunYaw, gunPitch), vlsF (32 cells) + vlsA (64 cells) with individual hatches
  (state vlsOpen: array of open cell ids, ids 0..31 fwd, 32..95 aft), ciwsF + ciwsA (Phalanx 1B: pedestal, radome, 6-barrel
  M61 gatling; state ciwsYaw [f, a], ciwsPitch [f, a], ciwsSpin rad), decoys (Mk 36 SRBOC / Nulka launchers), boats
  (RHIB + davits), hangar (twin doors), deck (flight deck markings, line prims), rails (lifelines, fine), bridge windows,
  yardarm antennas.
  anchors object HD.destroyer.A: stacks [2 exhaust mouths], vls(i) -> cell centre, ciws(st, i) -> muzzle,
  gun(st) -> muzzle, spy [4 face centres], heloSpot, stern, bow.

HD.helo()  MH-60R Seahawk. Origin on the ground under the rotor mast, nose +Z. state: rotor (rad), trotor (rad),
  droop (0 flight .. 1 at rest). parts: fuselage (cockpit glazing, doors), rotor (4 blades, 16.36 m disc, hub),
  tailrotor (4 blades, canted), tail (boom, pylon, stabilator), gear, sensors (nose turret, radome), pylons.

HD.fighter()  F/A-18E Super Hornet. Origin at mid-body, gear up. state: fan (rad), nozzle (0 closed .. 1 open), ab (0..1).
  parts: fuselage, lex, wings, tails (twin canted), stabs, canopy, intakes (caret intakes WITH visible first-stage fan
  faces: blades + spinner, turning with fan), nozzles (petals), pylons (+ optional stores).
  constants: HD.fighter.NOZZLES [2], HD.fighter.INTAKES [2].

HD.drone()  Orlan-10-class recon UAV (3.1 m span). Origin at mid-body. state: prop (rad), gimYaw, gimPitch.
  parts: fuselage, wing, tail, prop (2 blades), gimbal (EO ball under the nose), antennas. constants: HD.drone.PROP, .GIMBAL.
HD.catapult()  pneumatic launch rail on a trailer; state carriage (0..1 along the rail). constant: HD.catapult.RAIL (start, end).

HD.satellite()  Lotos-S1 / Kondor-class spacecraft. Origin at the bus centre, +Z = velocity, -Y = nadir.
  parts: bus, solar (2 wings, cell grids, hinges), reflector (deployable mesh dish: rim, ribs, feed boom), sensors
  (star trackers, antennas), thrusters.

HD.carrier()  Nimitz-class, lower detail (seen from far/above): flight deck + angled deck outline, island with radars,
  catapult tracks, elevators, a few parked aircraft (HD.fighter with fine:false or simpler). Origin midships waterline.

Budgets: HD.destroyer as wire < ~15k segments full detail (much less with fine:false); sampled at 0.35 m < ~200k points.
Small craft proportionally less.
