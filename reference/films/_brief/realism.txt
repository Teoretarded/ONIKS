INTERCEPTOR LAUNCH LOOK (visual smoothing only)
==============================================

The user flagged that the interceptors in the films leave the deck and swing over almost at once, which looks wrong.
This is an animation fix, done by eye, like an animator would: no speeds, turn loads, guidance or intercept geometry are
worked out.

1 Off the deck: a longer straight rise out of the cell, clearly vertical for the first second or so, slow at first and
  visibly accelerating (ease-in), so it reads as heavy.
2 The turn: a gentle, round bend over several seconds into the existing path; a long smooth arc (the classic rainbow smoke
  trail), never a hook or an elbow. Blend into the film's existing downstream path rather than re-deriving it.
3 Keep every meeting point and time as it is. If the smoother rise needs more time, start the launch a little earlier and
  shift its log line / tag / cue with it.
4 Smoke trails are laid along the path, so smoothing the path smooths the trail.
