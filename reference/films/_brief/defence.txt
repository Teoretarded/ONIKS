SHIP DEFENCE SERIES: long menu cinematics after the user's favourite of the original ten menus
============================================================================================

These are video-game menu cinematics for the ONIKS strategy game. Combat is shown the way a game trailer shows it: as
spectacle (motion, light, flashes, tracer, smoke, debris, short labels). Never as tactics: no guidance or targeting
logic, no countermeasure or counter-countermeasure reasoning, no damage mechanics, no weapon internals.

The favourite ("Terminal", Orbital style) in words, so nobody needs to open its source: the fight at a destroyer seen
from low on the sea past the ship toward the horizon. Three rounds come in low from the horizon; two interceptors leave
the ship's deck on smoke-ring trails; one round is stopped ~7 km out with a far flash; another weaves past its
interceptor; the ship's close-in gun (Phalanx) stops the third at ~480 m with a burst of tracer; the weaving one reaches
the hull: a yellow flash marks the breach. The camera looks up after the launches and settles back on the horizon. A
short engagement log on the right, newest line in yellow. Menu column on the left. Black, white hairlines, one yellow.

Every film in the series keeps those elements (the destroyer as the hero; rounds coming in low from the horizon;
interceptors off the deck with trails; far flashes; the close-in gun turning and firing tracer; an engagement log or tags;
a hit or a near miss) and adds its own big idea. Each is one continuous take of 150-180 s that loops seamlessly, with
the menu on screen, pure function of film time T, HD models, <= 16 ms/frame, sound via FILM.cue. Common rules:
_brief/films.txt. Models: common/hd_sea_air.js (HD.destroyer + HD.destroyer.A anchors: vls(i), ciws, ciwsDir, gun,
gunDir, spy, stacks, deckY...; states vlsOpen, ciwsYaw/ciwsPitch/ciwsSpin, gunYaw/gunPitch, sps; HD.carrier, HD.helo,
HD.fighter; HD.wire(model)), common/hd_land.js (HD.sm6, HD.mk72, HD.oniks, HD.oniksBooster). Never open ../menus/*.html.
Copy drawing code freely from the finished films in this folder: oc_underway_world.js / oc_underway_fast.js (wire sea,
wake, ship, radar arcs, climbing plumes, tracer streams, smoke, flashes), oa_scale_main.js, ob_long_exposure_core.js,
pa_picture_*.js / pa_picture_hit.js (scope, tracks, dot bursts), pb_survey_film.js (dot ship, tracer dots, dot burst,
smoke), pc_anatomy_*.js (X-ray scan, exploded view, tags).

Built in two stages by separate builders; the files carry over:
 A  Everything except the fighting: timeline, one-take camera path, chapters, menu/log/captions, sea, ships, the rounds
    flying their paths, the interceptors' flight paths as plain lines, the loop seam, performance. Clearly named empty
    hooks for the combat effects (e.g. launchFx(T), interceptFx(T), ciwsFx(T), hitFx(T)).
 B  The combat effects into those hooks, then polish every chapter, verify the seam and performance, pick 4 gallery
    moments.

ORBITAL (black, white hairline wireframe, one yellow accent, Inter + DM Mono, catalog labels, "Fig. N" captions)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OD  od_salvo.html       Salvo        (separate brief: _brief/combat.txt)
OE  oe_ring.html        Ring         The camera never leaves the destroyer: one slow continuous orbit around it (40-250 m,
    rising and falling), closing in on each system as it acts. Three raids, each bigger: two rounds (both stopped far
    out, flashes on the horizon); four (interceptors rippling out of the fore and aft cells, the 5-inch gun firing, a
    Phalanx kill close in); six from two bearings (everything firing, decoy rockets blooming into hairline clouds, both
    Phalanx mounts, one hit aft: fire, and the ship keeps fighting). Loop: the smoke drifts across the lens and the orbit
    comes out on a sister ship, calm, identical to frame 0 (like Underway's loop).
OF  of_task_force.html  Task Force   The whole group: carrier in the middle, cruiser and two destroyers around it. A raid
    of twelve from three bearings; the sky fills with a lattice of interceptor trails from every ship; the camera starts
    high over the formation, dives down through the lattice between the ships at mast height, rides along a destroyer's
    side as its Phalanx fires, and sees the destroyer on the threat axis take the hit meant for the carrier. The carrier
    turns and launches fighters through the drifting smoke. Pull back high; the formation re-forms calm = frame 0.
OG  og_bullet_time.html Bullet Time  The last twenty seconds of a defence stretched across the whole film with a variable
    clock (x0.02 .. x1, always shown): the camera moves freely through the nearly frozen fight: interceptor plumes as
    hanging cones, tracer streams as dashed lines in the air, fragments suspended, the last round a few metres from the
    hull with the Phalanx's tracer reaching for it. Near the end time ramps to real speed: bursts, a flash, and then the
    whole thing rewinds (like Long Exposure) back to the calm first frame.
OH  oh_storm.html       Storm        Night, a storm at sea: heavy swell (sea lines heaving), rain streaks, spray over the
    bow as the destroyer pitches, low cloud; lightning flashes that light every hairline at once for a moment. The raid
    comes in low through the rain; interceptor plumes glow inside the cloud layer; Phalanx tracer through the rain; a
    hit forward; the next lightning shows the damage; the storm rolls on and the loop returns to the calm-before.

POINT CLOUD (graphite, white LiDAR dots, lime = own / scans, coral = hostile, Geist + Geist Mono, tags, dotted boxes)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PD  pd_engagement.html  Engagement   (separate brief: _brief/combat.txt)
PE  pe_aegis.html       Aegis        The ship's own radar picture: the camera starts at a SPY array face; a 360° dot scope
    centred on the destroyer (sea clutter, the group, aircraft), the raid condensing out of uncertainty clouds on the
    horizon (coral), lime interceptor tracks leaving the ship, bursts of dots where they meet, the Phalanx's own radar as
    a dense dot cone for the last one; the camera rises to see the whole picture, then dives back to the array face.
PF  pf_gun_camera.html  Gun Camera   The close-in gun's own camera: framed through its sight (reticle, range, lag), dot sea
    and sky; tracking boxes on each incoming round on its true bounds, tracer dots streaming out from the bottom of the
    frame, rounds bursting into debris dots; between engagements the camera pulls back to show the Phalanx mount itself
    (HD.destroyer ciws sampled, barrels spinning) on the deck; four engagements, each different (single, pair, very low,
    weaving); the last one gets through on the far side and the camera turns to see the flash aft.
PG  pg_thermal.html     Thermal      A thermal imager's view in dots: white-hot stacks, plumes and muzzles; a warm hull; a
    cold speckled sea; interceptor launches that bloom and blind; barrel heat glow; the hit as a saturating bloom and
    debris glowing as it cools. Mid-film the view switches from white-hot to black-hot and back. Lime/coral overlays.
PH  ph_damage_scan.html Damage Scan  Combat meets Anatomy (pc_anatomy.html is the user's favourite film): the destroyer
    defends and takes a hit; then a lightning scan (dots pour down, the strike branches through the hull) sweeps the ship
    and the damaged section's systems float apart in an exploded view (superstructure blocks, a radar face, a VLS module,
    a Phalanx mount, a boat, mast sections), each tagged; they glide back together as the ship steams on, smoke thinning;
    the loop returns to the calm ship before the raid.
