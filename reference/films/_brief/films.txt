EXTENDED MENU CUTSCENES: common brief for every film builder
=============================================================

Context
-------
ONIKS is a strategy sim set on a coast: a Bastion battery (Oniks TELs, Monolith-B radar, Pantsir, drones) faces a
naval strike group offshore. For the main-menu overhaul the user reviewed ten animated menu prototypes in
renders/ui_overhaul/menus/: five ORBITAL (o1_ignition, o2_seaskim, o3_orbit, o4_chronograph, o5_terminal) and five
POINT CLOUD (p1_strike, p2_clutter, p3_coastline, p4_track, p5_confidence). They loved them, especially the radar scans.
Now they want, per style, THREE extended "menu cutscenes": each a long, continuous film stitched from that style's
concepts in a logical order, with invented filler that bridges the gaps, smooth with no cuts, cinematic, realistic,
"fucking epic", with much more detail on the models (thrusters, fan blades, rotors...). Each of the three must be
special and have its own unique idea. Each film keeps its style's look.

Where
-----
Your page: renders/ui_overhaul/films/<id>.html (optional helpers <id>_*.js next to it).
Served at http://localhost:8770/films/<id>.html (server root = renders/ui_overhaul; it is running,
do not start servers).
Load order:

Read first: common/film.js (runtime + camera/time helpers, API in its header comments), menus/common/*.js (the kit),
and every source menu your treatment names, in full: reuse their techniques and code freely (copy into your page).
kit/ and _models.html in this folder are an abandoned earlier attempt: mine for ideas, never load them.

Rules
-----
1. ONE CONTINUOUS TAKE. No cuts, no fades to black, no cross-dissolves between unrelated shots. Every transition is a
   camera move or an in-world match-move (follow an object from one place to the next, fly through a display into the
   world it shows, the map's lines becoming the terrain, a pull-back that reveals the next scene...). The film LOOPS
   seamlessly: design the ending so it flows back into the opening state (check T = duration-0.5 against T = 0.5).
2. Duration 150-180 s. 6-8 chapters in FILM.run({chapters}), short titles.
3. PURE FUNCTION OF FILM TIME. render(T) depends only on T: no Math.random, no accumulated state. Precompute
   trajectories at load (integrate the physics once into tables and look them up); particles (smoke rings, puffs,
   sparks, debris, tracers, spray) are analytic in their spawn time + a seeded per-particle rng index. Camera shake via
   FILM.shake / FILM.drift. This makes scrubbing, ?t= stills and the loop exact. FILM.path gives keyframed Hermite
   camera paths (loopable), FILM.warp gives eased time-warp ramps (film time -> sim time).
4. REALISM (visual). Real dimensions, speeds and timings so motion reads true. P-800 Oniks: 8.9 m, Ø 0.7 m; cold gas
   ejection from the vertical TLC, booster lights ~0.5 s after ejection, pitch-over, booster burns ~4-5 s then is
   ejected out of the ramjet nozzle, wings and fins unfold, ramjet cruise (shock diamonds in the plume); about Mach 2
   low over the sea. Arleigh Burke 155 m. Whenever the film runs faster than real time, show the rate on screen
   (e.g. "x8"). Labels must be true, and tracked boxes/brackets must actually fit their objects (the user checks this).
   Keep it a cinematic game backdrop: machinery, motion, light and sensors as spectacle, not tactics.
5. DETAIL. Use the HD models: shock-diamond exhaust, fan blades, spinning rotors and props, gatling barrels, hydraulic
   rams, folding wings, VLS hatches, turning radars. Bring the camera close enough, often enough, that it shows.
6. STYLE (non-negotiable; match the source menus exactly):
   ORBITAL: black, white hairline wireframe (facing-aware, depth-faded, the Wire renderer), exactly one accent: yellow
     highlighter #F4D23C used sparingly (our battery, one key object at a time, the selected menu row). Inter + DM Mono,
     catalog labels (ORB.cat: square + designation + barcode), "Fig. N" captions, thin leaders. No filled shapes and
     no glow blobs, except restrained bloom at ignitions and impacts.
   POINT CLOUD: graphite #0B0C0A, white LiDAR dots (PointBuf, max blend), one accent: lime #C6F432 for scans and
     identification. Geist + Geist Mono, tags (white/lime id chip + dark label), dotted boxes and underlines. Coral
     #FF6A3D only for hostile/warning, sparingly.
   The user hates anything that looks like a website template or "vibe coded", and never wants bright/white UI.
7. IT IS A MENU CUTSCENE: the game's five entries (STAGE.ITEMS through STAGE.menu, with blurbs) stay on screen, laid out
   like your style's menus; compose the film around them (keep the action out from under the menu, calm the menu side
   with a gradient like the source menus). H hides the UI (film.js) for a clean film. Keep other text minimal and
   diegetic: chapter caption ("Fig. N ..." / kick label), one live readout, tracked labels.
8. SOUND: synthesized via STAGE.SFX (tone / noise / rumble / crack), fired by FILM.cue(t, fn) so it only plays in real
   time (never on seeks).
9. PERFORMANCE: <= 16 ms per frame at 1920x1080. Measure FILM.bench(120, T) in the page (javascript) at the heaviest
   moments. Use {fine:false} for distant models, cache static geometry and samples, no allocations in hot loops.
10. OWNERSHIP: you edit only your <id>.html (and <id>_*.js). Never edit menus/**, common/*.js, _brief/*, other films.
   If you need a kit change, extend locally in your page and mention it in your report.

HD models
---------
common/hd_land.js (HD.tel, HD.telTube, HD.oniks, HD.oniksBooster, HD.radar, HD.pantsir, HD.sm6, HD.mk72) and
common/hd_sea_air.js (HD.destroyer, HD.helo, HD.fighter, HD.drone, HD.catapult, HD.satellite, HD.carrier) are being built
right now by two other agents, to the API in _brief/models.txt (read it). They are drop-in supersets of GEO.tel,
GEO.tubeFrame, GEO.oniks, GEO.radarTruck, GEO.interceptor, GEO.destroyer. Build your choreography against the GEO models
through a small adapter in your page, e.g.
  const MOD = { tel: () => (window.HD && HD.tel ? HD.tel() : GEO.tel()), ... };
and switch to HD as soon as the files exist (ls common/hd_*.js; HD.READY_LAND / HD.READY_SEA_AIR). Models that have no
GEO equivalent (helo, fighter, drone, satellite, pantsir...): stub them simply until the HD file lands. Before you
finish, every model in your film must be the HD one. They should land well before you finish; if not, keep polishing
and check again.

Review loop (mandatory)
-----------------------
Look at your frames, repeatedly. Use the built-in browser tools (mcp__Claude_Browser__*): open your OWN tab
(tabs_create), navigate it to
  http://localhost:8770/films/_shoot.html?s=<id>@t1,t2,t3
which writes films/shots/<id>_t<sec>.png (a '.' in t becomes '_') and sets the tab title to
"SHOT n/n" when done; then Read the PNGs. Load your page with ?t=SEC to check STAGE.err and console errors, and run
FILM.bench. Check every chapter (start, middle), every transition, and the loop seam. Iterate until every frame is one
you'd be proud to show. If the browser tools are not available to you, stop and report that immediately.

Final report (your last message, concise)
-----------------------------------------
Beat-by-beat with timestamps; the 4 best moments (T values: hero + 3) for the gallery; known rough edges; bench ms.
